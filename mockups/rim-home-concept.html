Your first visit

Knowing what will happen helps.

A first meditation group can feel unfamiliar. Here is the whole thing—no hidden expectations and no special vocabulary required.

  1. 01

    Choose in person or online.

    Come to our quiet center in Brookfield or join the same gathering on Zoom from home.

  2. 02

    Arrive a few minutes early.

    Someone will greet you. Chairs and cushions are ready; wear whatever lets you sit comfortably.

  3. 03

    Practice, listen, ask—or simply be quiet.

    Most gatherings include guided meditation and teaching. Participation is always invitational.

  4. 04

    Offer dana only if you want to.

    There is no admission price. Generosity makes the teaching available to the next person.

Ancient roots · Plain language

A tradition you can question.

We are rooted in the earliest Buddhist teachings and the Chan practice of Silent Illumination. We teach them as living practices—not beliefs you are asked to adopt, and not ideas that stand between you and your own experience.

You do not need to be Buddhist to practice here. Curiosity, honesty, and a willingness to pay attention are enough.

Sustained by generosity

No price at the door.

Teachers offer the dharma freely. Those who are able help sustain the center, the teachers, and access for the next person. Give money, time, care—or simply your sincere presence.
