# Rooted in Mindfulness — Calibrated Design Pass

This revision returns to the expressive Version 2 design and deliberately avoids a new visual concept.

## What remains intact

- The original transparent header and botanical wordmark
- Newsreader + DM Sans typography
- Soft organic gradients, circles, hills, and generous rounded surfaces
- The original homepage composition
- The category-led weekly schedule
- The multi-audience Programs architecture
- Public navigation and a distinct member entry point

## What changed

### Lower visual volume

Display sizes, card padding, hero height, and section spacing were reduced by roughly 8–12%. Body copy remains 16px or larger. The result should feel calmer without becoming minimal or generic.

### Page-specific heroes

Every public page now has its own emotional color atmosphere while using the same underlying layout:

- New Here: refuge and permission
- Programs: breadth and growth
- This Week: clarity and immediacy
- Teachings: parchment and concentration
- Community: layered relationship
- About: earth and roots
- Join: an open threshold
- Support: generosity moving outward
- Program Detail: a warm book-jacket treatment

### Programs

The multi-audience structure remains. Category color is now applied to the program family rather than arbitrarily to every individual card. This makes the palette meaningful:

- Weekly practice: warm sunrise
- Daily meditation: quiet water
- Study: neutral parchment
- Community: living green
- Retreats: clay and distance

Rows are shorter, headings are smaller, and practical facts remain visible without requiring filters or hidden controls.

### Usability

- One primary action remains dominant at each decision point.
- Familiar text links remain visible; important actions do not depend on icons alone.
- Program time, format, and commitment stay visible before opening detail pages.
- Mobile text remains at least 16px for ordinary body content.
- All earlier versions remain preserved separately.
