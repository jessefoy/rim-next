# Registration features of event-ticketing and nonprofit membership/event platforms

Research date: 2026-10-23. Sources are the vendors' official help centres and docs unless noted.

**Source-access notes (read first):**
- **Fetched directly and read in full:** Eventbrite help (several articles), Tito's current docs at go.tito.io (the newer product; the older help.tito.io articles describe "Tito Classic" and partly disagree with it), Humanitix help, Ticket Tailor help (two articles), Givebutter help (one article), CiviCRM User Guide (docs.civicrm.org).
- **Known only from search-engine summaries of official pages, not opened directly:** WildApricot (gethelp.wildapricot.com renders its article bodies client-side, so a direct fetch returned only titles), Arlo (support.arlo.co returned HTTP 403), Neon CRM (support.neoncrm.com failed with an expired TLS certificate), and Bloomerang (product pages plus one help article). Treat those as lower-confidence. Several of the Neon articles are old Zendesk articles (IDs from about 2016) and may be stale.
- Eventbrite help pages carry no visible dates, and search tooling flagged some as more than 1,000 days old. The interface may have moved.

---

## Key Question 1: What does each product do for registration types, capacity, waitlist, discounts, pay-what-you-want/donations, staff-entered registrations, groups, transfers, self-service, refunds, multi-session, recurring, check-in, exports, integrations, and roles?

### Takeaway
Every product separates **ticket/registration types with their own capacity** from the event's overall capacity, and every one lets staff add registrations by hand. Where they differ most: **waitlist automation** (Eventbrite, Ticket Tailor and Neon are manual; Tito, Humanitix, WildApricot, CiviCRM and Arlo can offer places automatically, and only Tito, CiviCRM, Eventbrite and Arlo document an offer that expires), **pay-what-you-want** (Tito and Humanitix document a buyer-set price with a minimum; Eventbrite has an any-amount "Donation" ticket; Givebutter tickets are fixed-price), and **role granularity** (Tito's six scoped roles and Eventbrite's custom roles are the most developed; CiviCRM's per-event ACLs are powerful but clumsy).

### Cited Findings

#### Eventbrite
- **Ticket types:** Paid, Free, or Donation (buyer pays any amount). Each type has its own quantity, sales window (or "start when another ticket type stops selling"), min/max per order, visibility (visible / hidden / hidden when not on sale, with a visibility schedule; hidden tickets are revealed with a promo code), sales channel ("At the door only" restricts to the Organizer app), description, dynamic pricing by date or by number sold, "Absorb fees", delivery (eTicket or Will Call), and a per-type check-in window. "Sections" each have their own capacity, and event capacity is their sum. — [Eventbrite: Create and edit ticket types](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/644100/how-to-create-custom-ticket-types/)
- **Donation tickets:** the buyer chooses the amount, which appears on the confirmation and ticket. A buyer "can't register donation tickets for free". There is a maximum of one of the same donation ticket per order, and donation tickets can't be used with reserved seating. Quantity is unlimited by default, so a donation ticket does not count toward capacity unless a quantity is set. To stop a donation from granting entry, turn off physical tickets. Ticketing fees apply to donations ("Absorb fees" is available); in the US there is no service fee, only 2.9% processing. **Donation ticket types are not available in California.** — [Eventbrite: Collecting donations](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/460357/)
- **Waitlist:** the "Waitlist trigger" fires when a specific (visible) ticket type sells out or the event reaches capacity. It collects name and email, and optionally phone. The list size can be capped (0 = no limit). There is an "Auto-response message" on joining. Attendees can't see their position. Not available with Reserved Seating. — [Eventbrite: Set up an event waitlist](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/811539)
- **Waitlist release is manual:** the organiser opens Manage Waitlist, selects people, and clicks "Release Tickets". The recipient gets a "Claim my spot" email and must register within the "Time to Respond" window. An expired entry "turns red", and the organiser must release to that person again (no automatic move to the next person is documented). One entry equals one ticket. Releasing can exceed total capacity. An entry can only be deleted after its response time has passed. The recipient can hand the spot to someone else by entering different attendee details. — [Eventbrite: Manually release tickets to the waitlist](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/817355/)
- **Order form:** custom questions under Order options > Order form, each required or optional. The "Each attendee" setting collects a name and email for every ticket. Without it, only the buyer's information exists. — [Eventbrite: Set up your event order form](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/246376/how-to-collect-information-from-all-event-attendees/); [Create custom questions](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/228823/how-to-create-custom-questions-for-attendees/)
- **Staff-entered registrations:** Manage Attendees > "Add Attendees". Choose an order type (e.g., "Complimentary" or "No payment necessary"), fill in the order form, and place the order. No fees are charged, but cards can't be processed (use the Organizer app to take a card). — [Eventbrite: Add attendees manually](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/874192/how-to-add-attendees-manually/)
- **Offline payment methods** (check, invoice, pay at event) exist; Eventbrite advises confirming payment before check-in. — [Eventbrite: Accept payments by check, invoice, or at the event](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/283889/how-to-accept-payments-by-check-invoice-or-at-the-event-with-offline-payments/)
- **Editing orders:** the organiser can edit attendee or buyer info (Manage Orders > Actions), but not billing info or ticket count (reduce the count with a partial refund). Per-attendee editing works only if the form collected "Each attendee". Attendees can't be added to an existing order. — [Eventbrite: Edit attendee information](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/544246/how-to-edit-attendee-information/)
- **Transfers:** the organiser can enable self-service "Registration Transfers" (Order Options). The attendee can switch to a different ticket type or event, but only on paid, public events using Eventbrite Payment Processing, not reserved seating, not password/invite-only, not yet started, and not checked in. Otherwise organisers can transfer manually. — same Eventbrite help results as above ([Edit attendee info](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/544246/how-to-edit-attendee-information/)). The transfer article itself was not opened; these details come from search summaries.
- **Refunds:** the organiser sets the refund policy. Attendees request refunds from their account where the policy allows, and the organiser should respond within 5 business days, with escalation to Eventbrite. A cancelled event must be refunded. Full or partial refunds are issued from Manage Orders or the Organizer app. Ticketing fees are generally non-refundable. — [Set and manage your refund policy](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/130304/how-to-set-a-refund-policy/); [Request a refund](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/721549/can-i-get-a-refund/); [Issue a full or partial refund](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/158301/how-to-refund-an-order/); [Is the Ticketing Fee refundable?](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/548304/is-the-eventbrite-fee-refundable/)
- **Email:** "Emails to attendees" (under Manage attendees) sends now or on a schedule, or a set number of days, hours or minutes before the start. Once queued, an email can't be edited. "All attendees" means people registered at send time. **Automatic reminders:** in-person events get one at 48 hours; online events get three (48 hours, 2 hours, 10 minutes). Both are editable and deletable. The custom order-confirmation text appears on the PDF ticket and in the confirmation email. — [Eventbrite: What emails attendees receive](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/748624/what-emails-will-attendees-automatically-receive/); [Edit or delete the automatic reminder](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/222665/how-to-create-or-disable-the-48-hr-reminder-email/); [Customize your order confirmation](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/385031/how-to-create-custom-order-confirmations/)
- **Roles:** four default roles (Owner, Admin, "Check-in attendees", "Check-in attendees and guests") plus custom roles built from a permissions glossary (event creation, marketing, orders & attendees, reporting, payouts & billing, organisation management). Check-in-only roles can't hold other roles. — [Manage roles and permissions](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/509534/how-to-manage-roles-and-permissions/); [Permissions Glossary](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/362073/permissions-definitions/)
- **Guest lists** (non-ticket guests) are managed by admins and owners, or by users with specific guest-list permissions. — [Create a guest list](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/569587/create-a-guest-list-and-manage-guests/)
- **Check-in:** the Organizer app (phone or tablet; QR scanning; can download the list and check in offline, syncing later) or a laptop browser. — [Set up the Organizer App](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/883605/set-up-the-organizer-app-on-your-phone-or-tablet/); [Check in from your computer](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/126937/how-to-use-a-laptop-to-check-in-attendees-at-your-event/); offline sync per [Eventbrite blog](https://www.eventbrite.com/blog/onsite-operations-tools-eventbrite/)
- **Recurring and multi-date events:** supported ("Set up a single event with multiple dates or times"). The waitlist works across all dates, with release by selected date. — [Eventbrite: multi-date events](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/454084/how-to-setup-a-multi-date-multi-location-event/); [Waitlist](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/811539)
- **Reports and exports:** orders reports can be customised and exported. — [Manage orders for a single event](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/855993/how-to-customize-and-export-an-orders-report/)
- **Payouts:** the default is a few days after the event; a payout schedule (weekly on Wednesdays, or the 1st and 15th) is available to organisers who have run a paid event before. Sources disagree on the default (3 vs. 5 days). — [Set up a payout schedule](https://eventbrite.com/support/articles/en_US/Troubleshooting/how-to-request-scheduled-payouts-for-your-events)

#### Tito (go.tito.io, the current product)
- **Ticket types:** a blank price means free; there are price tiers, volume prices, and "Booker chooses" (pay what you want). Quantity per type (blank = unlimited, 0 = shows "fully booked"). Each type can be on or off sale and have "its own time". Registration closes event-wide. "Choose where a ticket sells" (event page, audiences, widgets, portals, invitations). Ticket requests and approval ("Decide who gets in", "Run a ballot"). Waitlist. Sessions, items and donations can be included. Add-ons. A per-person cap. "Require a companion ticket". A one-free-ticket sign-up form. Bundles. — [Tito: Ticket types](https://go.tito.io/docs/events/ticket-types)
- **Pay what you want ("Booker chooses"):** a Minimum price (enforced) and an optional Suggested price that pre-fills the box. A minimum of 0 lets people come free, and checkout skips payment. Everyone in one order pays the same price per type. Discount codes apply to the chosen price, even below the minimum. These tickets can't be bundled. Invitations, waitlist places, admin orders and API orders charge the minimum unless a price is set. **Reporting shows how many sold at the minimum, between minimum and suggestion, at the suggestion, and above it, plus average price.** — [Tito: Pay what you want](https://go.tito.io/docs/events/pay-what-you-want)
- **Donations** can be an "offering" or an add-on alongside tickets. — [Tito: Ticket types](https://go.tito.io/docs/events/ticket-types); [Tito: Offerings](https://go.tito.io/docs/events/offerings)
- **Sessions ("offerings"):** three kinds of offering (Sessions, Items, Donations). A session can run multiple times, and each time has its own capacity and its own on-sale switch. Attendees choose a session, or alternatives (morning *or* afternoon). — [Tito: Offerings](https://go.tito.io/docs/events/offerings)
- **Holds:** selected tickets are held 15 minutes by default ("Hold tickets for"), renewing up to 2 hours if no one else is waiting. — [Tito: How booking works](https://go.tito.io/docs/getting-started/how-booking-works)
- **Waitlist (current product): automatic or manual per ticket type.** "Open a waitlist and email people in order" emails people in join order, one person every interval set under "Email one person every". "Open a waitlist and choose who gets each place" is manual ("Offer to the next person…" / "Offer a place…"). **Each offer holds the place for a chosen time.** If not taken, the person returns to their old position. Offers can be withdrawn. While anyone waits, freed places stay off the public page ("Fully booked"). Places free up on cancel, refund, unpaid order, move, or a capacity increase. Each waitlist email has a leave-the-list link. Position is visible to the organiser only. Export is in join order. "Message these people". Workshops can have their own per-option waitlists. Waitlist places must be paid by card or PayPal (no bank transfer). — [Tito: Waitlist](https://go.tito.io/docs/events/waitlist)
  - *Conflict:* the older Tito Classic help says offers are manual only, with an `{{expires}}` time on a one-time `{{rsvp_link}}`, and rejection is silent. — [Tito Classic: Waiting lists](https://help.tito.io/en/articles/2003015-waiting-lists). The go.tito.io page is the current product.
- **Questions:** types are short or long answer, yes/no, pick one, pick any (with an optional write-in), checkbox, date, number, phone, country, and file upload. Eleven ready-made questions (e.g., dietary). Questions live in a library and are attached per ticket type (or "Add to all"), or to sessions, portals, request forms and the order form. "Required" is set per ticket type. Each question's fixed "export name" is its CSV column. Related pages: "Fix an answer a booker gave at checkout", "See what people answered". — [Tito: Questions](https://go.tito.io/docs/events/create-a-question)
- **Booker vs. holder:** the booker gets receipts. Each ticket of an assignable type can have a named holder entered at checkout or later. Orders of 10+ tickets can upload holders from a spreadsheet. Each holder receives their own ticket email and code. Payment details go only to the booker. — [Tito: How booking works](https://go.tito.io/docs/getting-started/how-booking-works)
- **Attendee management (staff):** Add (one at a time, from a file, or paste). Edit name, email or answers. **Reassign** by changing the email; choose whether the new holder gets the email and whether the old address is told its link stopped working. Move to another ticket type, session option, or event while keeping references. "Refund or cancel…" per ticket, with a choice of what happens to the money. Bulk "Cancel tickets" returns places to sale with no refund and no email. Resend the ticket email with a one-time note. "Ask for the missing details". Export CSV or Excel. A per-ticket activity log. Name badges. — [Tito: Attendees](https://go.tito.io/docs/events/attendees)
- **Check-in:** named check-in lists (one per door). Each list covers all tickets or a filter (session/offering, ticket types). A ticket can be checked in on one list and not another. The "Still to arrive" view. Message people in the current view. Colour-coded results (green / amber already-in / red invalid), undo, and history. Browser camera scanning on supported browsers (not iPhone or Safari); otherwise search by name or reference. Refunded tickets are voided automatically. — [Tito: Check-in](https://go.tito.io/docs/events/check-in)
- **Roles:** six roles (Owner, Admin, Staff, Door, Viewer, Copy reviewer), each scoped to the whole account, named series, named events, or "Every event". Staff handle orders, attendees, check-in, badges and messages, but **no refunds, data export, or settings**. Door is check-in only. Viewer is read-only. Capabilities can be narrowed by hand ("Custom access"). No one can grant more than they hold. — [Tito: Team](https://go.tito.io/docs/account/team)
- **Other docs sections exist** for Discounts, Messages (email attendees), Widgets, Refunds, Bundles, Portals (allocations), Reports, Contacts and Connections (integrations). They were not opened. — [Tito docs index](https://go.tito.io/docs)

#### Humanitix
- **Ticket selection:** ticket types and quantities, donations, discount codes (typed or applied via a unique link), **access codes that reveal hidden tickets** (preferential tiers, allocations, complimentary), date/time selection for timed or recurring events, ticket groups, packaged tickets (e.g., a table of 10), multi-page checkout with add-ons, and seat maps. — [Humanitix: The ticket buyer journey](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/13548552-the-ticket-buyer-journey-on-humanitix)
- **No account needed.** Name, email and mobile are always asked (mobile can be optional). Checkout questions can be per order (in the orders report) or per ticket (in the attendee report; this can collect a unique name and email per ticket). They can target specific ticket types, be required, or **be asked post-checkout**. Express Apple Pay / Google Pay checkout is hidden if required questions exist. — [Humanitix: buyer journey](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/13548552-the-ticket-buyer-journey-on-humanitix); [Collect attendee information with checkout questions](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/8950849-collect-attendee-information-with-checkout-questions)
- **Payment options:** card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, **invoice payment** (tickets reserved, buyer receives bank details), **payment plans** (weekly or monthly instalments), and buy-now-pay-later (Afterpay, Zip). — [Humanitix: buyer journey](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/13548552-the-ticket-buyer-journey-on-humanitix)
- **Flexible pricing ticket type:** "+ flexible pricing" with its own capacity. Either "Any amount" (with an optional min/max) or a "List of prices". — [Humanitix: Flexible pricing ticket type](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/9678580-how-to-create-a-flexible-pricing-ticket-type)
- **Donations at checkout:** an optional amount added to the order, paid to the host. Donations don't count toward capacity and produce no ticket. One donation option per event. The default is any amount over zero, or a range, or a list of suggested amounts. Tax-deductible receipts are available once charity details are entered. A booking fee applies at the not-for-profit rate. An "additional donations" report is exportable to CSV. — [Humanitix: Collect donations for your own cause](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/8905635-collect-donations-for-your-own-cause); [Issue tax-deductible receipts](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/8906249-issue-tax-deductible-receipts-for-donations-or-ticket-purchases); [How to find a list of donations](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/9970514-how-to-find-a-list-of-donations)
- **Waitlist:** per ticket type (blank = all types). **Automatic offers:** set a minimum number of available tickets to trigger offers (default 3). The first guest is emailed 30 minutes after the threshold is reached, then the next every 30 minutes (interval fixed). When everyone has been offered, tickets reopen to the public. **Manual offers** to selected guests are also possible. **Tickets are not reserved for the offered person** (first come, first served); no expiry is documented. Joining collects name, email, mobile, ticket types wanted and quantity. Manual adds need only name and email; there is no bulk upload. The organiser is alerted only when the first person joins. — [Humanitix: Enable and manage a waitlist](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/8892176-enable-and-manage-a-waitlist-for-your-event)
- **Manual orders (staff-entered):** orders/refunds > "+ new manual order". The payment type is complimentary, cash, or bank deposit (no card charge). Choose the date and quantity, and enter names, emails and ticket-level info. Placeholder details can be edited later. — [Humanitix: Create complimentary tickets and offline sales with a manual order](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/8896997-create-complimentary-tickets-and-offline-sales-with-a-manual-order)
- **Check-in:** the free "Humanitix for Hosts" app (QR scan or search by name or order ID; several devices sync; works offline after the first login) or the browser (manage attendees > attendees). Check-out is supported. The report downloads as CSV. Day-of sales are possible in the app. — [Humanitix: How to check in your attendees](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/8896951-how-to-check-in-your-attendees); [Humanitix for Hosts app](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/13753417-how-to-use-the-humanitix-for-hosts-app); [Sell tickets on the day](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/8892770-sell-tickets-on-the-day-with-the-humanitix-for-hosts-app)
- Buyers can add tickets to an existing order (article exists, not opened). — [Can a buyer add additional tickets to an existing order?](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/9772399-can-a-buyer-add-additional-tickets-to-an-existing-order)

#### Ticket Tailor
- **Pay what you want:** done by creating several ticket types, each with a different suggested price (no single free-entry price field was found). — [Ticket Tailor FAQs](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/collections/44875-faqs) (per search summary)
- **Donations at checkout:** switched on in event settings, with a title, description, and optional suggested amount that pre-fills the field. **Ticket Tailor charges no fee on donations** (the payment processor still does). At least one ticket type (it can be free) is required. UK Gift Aid is supported. — [Ticket Tailor: How to accept donations](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/950984-how-to-accept-donations-for-your-event); [How to accept Gift Aid](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/5323327-how-to-accept-gift-aid)
- **Waitlist: per event and manual.** Tick "Activate waitlist" (advanced settings). It can show only when no tickets are available. Sign-ups give an email address. **Notification is manual:** "Create Broadcast" from the sign-ups page; notified people move to "Notified signups". CSV export with "Mark new signups as notified after export". No offer expiry or per-type waitlist is documented. A pre-sale waitlist works by hiding ticket types. — [Ticket Tailor: Set up a waitlist](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/2748053-how-to-set-up-a-waitlist-for-your-event); [Waitlist before your event goes on sale](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/10079664-how-to-set-up-a-waitlist-before-your-event-goes-on-sale); [Waitlist for all time slots in one day](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/5496620-can-i-add-a-waitlist-for-all-of-my-time-slots-in-one-day)
- **Cancel, refund and move:** organisers cancel or refund orders. Moving an order to another event = cancel with full refund + rebook, with any price difference collected separately. — [How to cancel or refund an order](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/950960-how-to-cancel-or-refund-an-order); [Move an order to another event](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/6021872-how-to-move-an-order-to-another-event-in-your-box-office)
- **Self-serve** (see Key Question 3), plus ticket types and groups, a check-in app, and multi-checkout across events. — [Manage Ticket types and Groups](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/948763-how-to-manage-ticket-types-and-groups); [Check-in app](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/5151698-how-to-check-in-your-attendees-with-the-check-in-app-at-your-event); [Multi-checkout](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/13193667-how-to-add-multi-checkout-so-buyers-can-add-tickets-from-multiple-events-to-their-cart)

#### Givebutter (Events)
- **Tickets are fixed-price or free**; the help centre says custom ("Other") amounts are only for donations and "cannot" be entered for tickets. An Event campaign's checkout offers tickets and then an optional donation step with up to 18 preset levels plus "Other". — [Givebutter: Create and edit tickets](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/3661362-how-to-create-and-edit-tickets); [Customize donation amounts](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/2859751-how-to-customize-donation-amounts); [Submit a donation](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/1726570-how-to-submit-a-donation-to-a-givebutter-campaign)
- **Capacity:** per-ticket quantity is self-serve, but **total event capacity requires contacting Support**. — [Givebutter: Set total Event capacity](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/2859749-how-to-set-total-event-capacity)
- **Promo codes, ticket bundles, seating** exist. — [Promo codes](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/2799365-how-to-use-promo-codes); [Ticket bundles](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/9650325-how-to-create-and-sell-ticket-bundles); [Seating](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/11731317-how-to-create-event-seating-arrangements)
- **Managing sold tickets:** Transactions > Tickets lists all tickets with check-in status and who scanned them. **Reassign** requires creating the new holder as a contact first, is admin-only, and sends no email (resend afterwards). Custom-field answers are editable. Resend to the original or a new email. **Revoke** invalidates a ticket and returns it to the pool, cannot be undone, and does not refund. Ticket type can't be changed and partial refunds aren't possible. Filters with AND/OR logic; exports follow the filters. — [Givebutter: Manage sold tickets](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/5497720-how-to-manage-sold-tickets)
- **Refunds** must be funded from the same campaign's balance. — [Givebutter: Issue a refund](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/7109570-how-to-issue-a-refund)
- **Check-in:** a QR code plus a 5-digit ticket number per PDF ticket, scanned or searched in the mobile app (by name or number). Status shows as a green checkmark. — [Givebutter: Scan tickets and check guests in](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/2852270-how-to-scan-tickets-and-check-guests-in)
- **Waitlist:** no Givebutter waitlist feature was found in the help centre; only a blog post recommends having one. — [Givebutter blog: event registration form](https://givebutter.com/blog/event-registration-form). Treat as **not found**, not as confirmed absent.

#### WildApricot (association/membership) — lower confidence (search summaries of gethelp.wildapricot.com)
- **Simple vs. Advanced events:** only Advanced events have ticket (registration) types, guests and limits; Simple events get one RSVP type. — [WildApricot: Ticket types & settings](https://gethelp.wildapricot.com/en/articles/103-advanced-events-ticket-types-and-settings); [Setting up an advanced event](https://gethelp.wildapricot.com/en/articles/30-setting-up-an-advanced-event)
- **Registration-type fields** (from the Admin API schema): Name, Description, BasePrice, **GuestPrice**, Availability, **RegistrationCode** (code-protected type), **AvailableForMembershipLevels**, AvailableFrom/Through, MaximumRegistrantsCount, GuestRegistrationPolicy, **CancellationBehaviour, CancellationDaysBeforeEvent**, IsWaitlistEnabled. — [WildApricot registration type schema via apis.io (third-party mirror of the official API schema)](https://apis.io/json-structure/wildapricot/wildapricot-event--registration--type-structure/)
- **Member vs. guest pricing:** types can be restricted to everyone, members, or specific membership levels. Lapsed, overdue or pending members don't qualify for members-only types. ## Key Question 2: How do online (Zoom) events deliver join links, and to whom?

### Takeaway
Three models: **(1) one shared link in the confirmation email** (Ticket Tailor; Humanitix's simplest option); **(2) a gated "online event page"** reachable only via a ticket, with the link inside (Eventbrite, Humanitix Virtual Event Hub); **(3) per-registrant unique Zoom registrant links**, which also enable automatic attendance (Arlo; Eventbrite when Zoom registration is enabled). Gating by ticket type and time-release before the start are common.

### Cited Findings
- **Eventbrite:** attendees get a link to the **online event page** in their confirmation email and "must join from the online event page"; no manual or dial-in joining. By default every ticket type gets access, with restriction possible via "Ticket access" (by ticket type, add-on, or date/time). Joining is allowed 30 minutes before by default (adjustable). Several Zoom events per page. **Auto check-in when Zoom registrations are enabled**, which fails if the Zoom email differs from the Eventbrite email. **The Zoom integration doesn't work with recurring events** (workaround: paste the link under "Share additional content"), and the page can't be customised per date. Title, description and date changes sync to Zoom. — [Eventbrite: Zoom integration](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/858681/)
- **Eventbrite** sends three automatic reminders for online events (48 hours, 2 hours, 10 minutes). — [Eventbrite: What emails attendees receive](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/748624/what-emails-will-attendees-automatically-receive/)
- **Humanitix:** the simplest method is a link in the confirmation email or a later email campaign. Alternatively, the **Virtual Event Hub**, a page only valid ticket holders can open via the "view access link" in the confirmation or digital ticket. **Each link is unique and works for one person or device at a time.** Hub elements (e.g., a Zoom URL) can be restricted by ticket type or date and scheduled to appear n days or hours before. Opening the link **auto-checks the attendee in**. A reminder email can use the `@viewvirtualeventhub` shortcut. Hybrid events use separate in-person and online ticket types. — [Humanitix: Virtual event hub](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/8896988-event-guide-deliver-your-online-event-with-the-virtual-event-hub); [Online or webinar event guide](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/8913822-event-guide-online-or-webinar-event); [Hybrid events](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/8913940-event-guide-hybrid-events)
- **Ticket Tailor:** mark the event online, paste the Zoom invite link into "Online event links". The **online version of the confirmation email includes it automatically**. Reminders are manual via Broadcast. For Zoom webinars, Zapier can add buyers as webinar registrants. — [Ticket Tailor: Run online events using Zoom](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/3832225-how-to-run-online-events-using-zoom-and-ticket-tailor); [Set up an online event](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/3832434-how-to-set-up-an-online-or-virtual-event)
- **Arlo:** the Zoom integration gives **each registrant their own join URL in registration emails** (the link works for the first person who uses it). **Attendance is recorded automatically** (more than 5 minutes in the meeting, including the waiting room) if the meeting ends within 1 hour of the scheduled end. It doesn't overwrite existing attendance. It needs a unique email per registrant, Zoom "Registration: Required" on, and "Require authentication to join" off. A host can't be a registrant. — [Arlo: Zoom integration overview](https://support.arlo.co/hc/en-gb/articles/900000750923-Zoom-integration-overview); [Zoom troubleshooting and FAQs](https://support.arlo.co/hc/en-gb/articles/900000760526-Zoom-troubleshooting-and-FAQs)
- **Givebutter:** PDF tickets are emailed after purchase except for "Digital events". No virtual-link delivery article was found. — [Givebutter: Manage sold tickets](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/5497720-how-to-manage-sold-tickets) (per search summary)

### Inferences
- Per-registrant links (Arlo) give automatic attendance, but they depend on unique emails and Zoom registration. That collides with Zoom's registration rate limits and with households sharing one email, a trade-off for RIM.
- The gated-page model (Eventbrite, Humanitix) keeps the raw Zoom link out of email and lets one stable URL serve recurring sessions. Eventbrite's own integration notably breaks on recurring events.
- Releasing the link n minutes before the start (Eventbrite: 30 minutes; Humanitix: scheduled) is a common control.

### Gaps
- Tito, WildApricot, Neon, Bloomerang and CiviCRM: no official Zoom or online-link delivery docs found in this pass.
- Whether waitlisted (not yet registered) people ever receive links: not documented anywhere checked.

## Key Question 3: What can attendees do themselves (manage booking, edit answers, cancel, transfer, add to calendar)?

### Takeaway
The norm is a **no-login "manage order" link** in the confirmation email, with each self-service action switchable by the organiser: edit details or answers, cancel (with refund rules and an n-days-before cutoff), and reschedule or transfer to another date. Calendar and wallet adds are common. Account-based membership tools put this in a profile page instead.

### Cited Findings
- **Humanitix (no login):** a "manage order" link offers update order details (edit checkout answers; on by default, can be disabled), change seats, self refund or cancel within host-set percentage and period limits (off by default; otherwise "contact host"), manage a payment plan, and resell tickets. Tickets go to Apple, Google or Samsung Wallet and PDF. — [Humanitix: The ticket buyer journey](https://help.humanitix.com/en/articles/13548552-the-ticket-buyer-journey-on-humanitix)
- **Ticket Tailor ("Manage tickets" button; box office Settings > Self-serve):** view, print and resend confirmations and ticket codes. **Reschedule to another occurrence of the same recurring event** (default up to 10 times; all tickets move together). **Cancel and optionally refund** (no refund / to original method / **credit to a voucher**), optionally keeping booking or transaction fees and **donations**, with a days-before cutoff and no partial cancel. "Order management terms" shows the refund policy. Not available for past events, pending payments, offline or operator payments, and similar cases. — [Ticket Tailor: Enable self-serve order management](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/8975333-how-can-i-enable-self-serve-order-management-for-my-ticket-buyers); [Why can't my buyer refund via self-serve?](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/9516408-why-can-t-my-ticket-buyer-issue-their-own-refund-via-self-serve); [Add a refund policy](https://help.tickettailor.com/en/articles/4364067-how-can-i-add-a-refund-policy-to-my-event)
- **Tito:** each order has a private link (no account). The booker can change their email, "Add to this order" (more tickets or add-ons, with promo codes), name holders (including a spreadsheet upload for 10+), download a PDF and a "who's coming" CSV, and download the receipt. Each holder has their own ticket page with Wallet buttons. Organisers can trigger "Ask for the missing details". Waitlisters can leave the list via a link. — [Tito: How booking works](https://go.tito.io/docs/getting-started/how-booking-works); [Tito: Attendees](https://go.tito.io/docs/events/attendees); [Tito: Waitlist](https://go.tito.io/docs/events/waitlist)
- **Eventbrite:** attendees can request refunds from their account where the policy allows, and transfer to another ticket type or event if the organiser enables "Registration Transfers" (with eligibility limits). Waitlist recipients can give their spot to someone else at claim time. — [Request a refund](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/721549/can-i-get-a-refund/); [Manually release tickets to the waitlist](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/817355/); transfer details per search summaries of [Edit attendee info](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/544246/how-to-edit-attendee-information/)
- **CiviCRM:** a self-service cancel or transfer link in the confirmation email, closing n hours before the start. It covers the lead booker only. — [CiviCRM: Online event registration](https://docs.civicrm.org/user/en/latest/events/online-event-registration/); [CRM-19002](https://issues.civicrm.org/jira/browse/CRM-19002)
- **WildApricot:** registrants view registrations by default. Cancellation only if enabled on the ticket type, with a days-before limit. People can leave a waitlist from "My event registrations". — [WildApricot: Ticket types & settings](https://gethelp.wildapricot.com/en/articles/103-ticket-types-settings); [Event waitlists](https://gethelp.wildapricot.com/en/articles/3)
- **Arlo:** the customer portal manages registrations, shows upcoming courses and materials, and offers certificate download. Self-cancel (n days before; doesn't touch refunds, no admin alert) and self-transfer to another date of the same course. — [Arlo: Customer portal end-user experience](https://support.arlo.co/hc/en-gb/articles/360028058632-Customer-portal-end-user-experience); [Registration policies](https://support.arlo.co/hc/en-gb/articles/115005325123-Registration-policies-self-cancellation-self-transfers-and-course-terms-and-conditions)
- **Bloomerang:** the product tour says attendees can add or edit ticket info and use wallet QR tickets. — [Bloomerang product tour](https://get.bloomerang.co/product-tours/event-management/)

### Inferences
- "Cancel → refund as credit or voucher" and "keep the donation on cancel" (Ticket Tailor) are directly relevant to dana. A self-cancel that frees the seat for an automatic waitlist (Tito, CiviCRM, Arlo) closes the loop without the registrar.
- Arlo's self-cancel silently skipping refunds and admin notice is a cautionary pattern: self-service must still tell the registrar.

### Gaps
- Add-to-calendar (.ics) was not explicitly documented in any page read, apart from wallet passes. Calendar support is likely but unverified.
- Givebutter and Neon attendee self-service: not found.

### Inferences
- The features most directly matched to RIM's situation (dana, a part-time registrar, volunteer teams, Zoom, guests without accounts): pay what you want with a suggested amount and a $0 floor plus the "who paid what" distribution (Tito); a donation kept on cancellation and refund-to-credit (Ticket Tailor); an automatic waitlist with a timed hold (Tito, CiviCRM, Arlo); a no-login manage link (Humanitix, Tito); scoped Door or Staff roles (Tito); session-level attendance for courses (Arlo); a gated online page with a release window or per-person links (Eventbrite, Humanitix, Arlo).
