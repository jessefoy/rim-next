# The RIM Session Room — Retired

> **This document is retired (June 2026).** It described the old in-browser "RIM
> Session Room" (built on LiveKit). Sessions have **moved to Zoom** — "RIM
> orchestrates, Zoom is the room" — so none of the controls described in the old
> version exist anymore. The full historical walkthrough is preserved in git
> history if it's ever needed.

**For hosting RIM online sessions, the current guide is
[ZOOM_HOST_GUIDE.md](ZOOM_HOST_GUIDE.md)** — signing in, getting into Zoom, and
claiming your host tools. Two short companions go with it:

- **Once You're Hosting** — the handful of in-Zoom host tools you'll actually use
  (mute, co-host, spotlight the teacher, recording, ending the session).
- **What It Means to Host at RIM** — a one-page note on the relational heart of
  the role.

Nothing changes about how you start: you click **Join** from your RIM dashboard,
and RIM walks you straight into Zoom. You still don't need a Zoom account. 🌿
