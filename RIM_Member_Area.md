## Design decisions

**The member area joins the public design system (2026-10-09, phase 1 of 3).** Jesse: *"I would like the community or member area to conform to the rest of the site's design."* The decision: the rail and the shell stay structural (members need a stable personal rail), and the vocabulary becomes the public one. Phase 1 covered the personal pages (My Home and Upcoming, My Profile, My Teams, Community Care, A Handful of Leaves, welcome and reactivation): the page header takes the quiet hero's tier (a label-coloured uppercase eyebrow for the date, a heading-coloured serif title, a hairline beneath); section labels take the public eyebrow; every surface is white with a 1px `--rim-rule` border, a 12px radius and no shadow (the Today card's wrapper went transparent so each session and the later-today list are their own bordered surfaces; the My Teams list is one bordered list of rows; the Handful card, the empty states, the host welcome and the profile sections are bordered); the standalone links on My Home are `pp-btn` / `pp-btn--ghost` (Your upcoming programs, Full schedule, Browse programs, Back to My Home, the Handful card's two doors); the member buttons that live in client components (Join, the host welcome, Save profile, the welcome submit) carry `pp-btn`'s metrics by rule; sentence links in member reading take the public 600-weight thin-underline rule; the raw hex colours in the db2-, today-, host-welcome, profile-header and welcome-error rules became tokens. The block sits just before the AUTHENTICATED READABILITY contract and sets colour, surface, spacing and weight only; sizes stay with the contract. Record of the public vocabulary: `RIM_Public_Pages.md` ("The public refresh", "Links, two kinds"). **Phase 2 (same day):** the team side, scoped to `.hub-ws-layout`: the rail's active item and badge on tokens, label eyebrows; every team page header on the quiet-hero tier (Team Home, Conversations, a thread, Members, Activity, the trash, Files) with a hairline beneath; bordered 12px surfaces on the home cards, attention list, pinned threads, conversation rows and the original post, the compose and manage panels, the members list, the activity list, trash rows, the Finder list, a file's header, document, panel and comments, the dialogs and menus; Team Home's Links as a bordered row list; the `.btn` family, the welcome button, New conversation, Share, the dialog and file-page buttons, the editor save/cancel and the row actions on the one button family (filled, ghost, danger, 48px pills; row actions 44px); the back links on a thread and a file as ghost pills; sentence links in the welcome, orientation, posts and comments on the public rule; the unread gradient, the active-card gradient and the tinted hex chips replaced by tokens. **Phase 3 (same evening):** the tools and administration, scoped to `.tools-shell` and `.admin-ui`: the quiet-hero tier on every tool and admin page header (the Program Manager list gained one; it had none), label eyebrows on table heads, section labels and form labels, bordered 12px surfaces on tables, cards, the editor's tabs and card and sticky actions, dialogs and panels, inputs at the public form's shape, badges and statuses as pills, and the one button family on every tool and admin button (the Program Manager's "+ Add Program", the editor's Save/Cancel, "← Programs" and "View program page", the table row actions, the Scheduler's pills and row actions, the Course Manager's buttons and row link, the registry's "+ Add member", the email templates' Edit). Tables and forms keep their density. **All three phases were measured signed in through Jesse's Chrome (Claude in Chrome)** at 1500 and in a 390px same-origin frame: no overflow on any of the twenty signed-in routes checked, every button at 44px or more; the few things the measurement caught were fixed the same evening.


Jesse’s community includes people who are not comfortable with technology. Keep home pages minimal: show the next useful action, then clear destinations. Content volume belongs inside the destination, not in expanding home feeds. My Home has no team-message feed. Team Home (2026-10-13, every team) is the team's name and one line, one card for the team's primary tool, personal attention, then quiet destinations; the newcomer welcome, guidance, pinned conversations, schedule overview and disclosures are retired. Team membership is a directory rather than an ever-growing sidebar.

All first-party authenticated surfaces use `public/css/custom.css` as their design authority: primary UI 16px, metadata 15px, reading content 18px, shared heading/control/focus treatment, 44px control targets. Member, admin and apps share the contract. Provider-rendered Google content retains its provider rendering. Shared rules do not establish that every signed-in screen has been visually verified.

## Connections and invariants

- Dashboard presentation depends on offering kind, registration state, session timing and host/teacher early entry. Preserve the existing gates and timed refresh. Exclude pending-payment/cancelled registrations as before. Since 2026-10-20 My Home lists no upcoming registrations; it reads them only to place a registered in-person session in Today. An open voluntary gift is reached through the dana-reminder email's signed link to the register page. Any rebuild does not restore the retired `/account/programs` self-service management feature.
- Routine `earlyArrivalMessage` is the Session note: one quiet italic line with the session on My Home (2026-10-09; it was a Good to know disclosure). Public catalog and weekly schedule retain `specialAnnouncement` Updates; their shared `ProgramCardNotices` no longer accepts routine notes. Program editing and transactional email sources are unchanged.
- Profile writes keep their existing server action, `/api/account/bio`, `/api/account/avatar` and `/api/upload`. Avatar removal now reports HTTP failure instead of appearing successful. The user-bio editor remains the message variant.
- Community agreements share one text across join, welcome, registration, public agreements and member care. Moving the reading page does not change consent capture. Since 2026-09-25 that text is the Our Shared Vision frame (`COMMUNITY_SHARED_VISION_TITLE` + `COMMUNITY_AGREEMENTS_LEAD_IN`) and three agreements; the fourth became the frame.
- My Teams follows the existing status-blind read door. Paused membership visibility is distinct from write authority. Manage RIM is a header destination for ADMIN/REGISTRAR, not a new permission grant.
- Hub queries, app installs and app links keep the resource hub context, including `?hub=`. Home rendering never switches behavior by a hardcoded hub slug.
- Personal file favorites/colors/sort are stored by authenticated member and place; shared pins require existing file write authority. They never change Drive colors, ordering or grants.
- `useNavigationDrawer` handles Escape, focus containment, scroll lock and focus restoration for account/hub drawers. The lock sets `overflow: hidden` on `<html>`, not `<body>`: the global `html { overflow-x: clip }` stops a body lock from reaching the viewport (found live on the public nav sheet, 2026-09-28). Header account menu also closes on Escape, outside pointer and navigation.

## Today and the teams (2026-10-15/16)

- **Hosting comes from the program's hosting team only** (early entry, "Enter Zoom as host", a session in Today because you host it). A greeter or AV sign-up is not hosting.
- **A supporting-team duty that day is a quiet line in Today**, in the team's words ("You're greeting"), with no button: the work happens in the hall. It joins the program's own row when there is one, and stands alone otherwise (`dashboard/page.tsx`, the duty rows; checked by the harness).
- **Join stays until the door closes** (the end plus the late grace), as the door does (Jesse, 2026-10-15).

## Notifications (2026-10-20)

Jesse: *"in one's general member area, a notification section that can be updated as we go… notifications from all kinds of sources because some people don't even have hubs… like text meets email, where there's the right information with a dropdown… truly informative and useful, not just having these features with checkboxes to organize… and where they can go into the hub when they click them."*

- **`/account/notifications`**, in the personal rail under My Home with an unread count (also in the phone menu), and one line on My Home ("You have 3 new notifications") when there's news.
- **Every notice is written once** (`lib/notifications.ts`): each email sender passes a `notice` to `sendTemplatedEmail` (title, details, an app link, a source: a team, Programs, Your account), which records it before deciding the email. The four rotation emails, which bypass the template manager, record directly. Staged accounts that have never signed in get none, as with the emails. A team member whose team emails a coordinator has switched off still gets the notice, not the email (Jesse, 2026-10-20: the switch stops emails only; `getHubNotificationRecipients(…, { withMuted: true })` marks them `muted`, and the senders pass `noticeOnly`).
- **Text meets email:** each row is one line (where from, what happened, the time); tapping it opens the details and one button named for where it goes ("Open the conversation", "Open the schedule"); notices about one thing on one day fold together ("2 more"). Opening marks read. Filters: the shared grammar, All · Unread, **From** (which team) and **View** (what kind). **Categorized like team Updates** (2026-10-20, Jesse: "just like in the team hubs… like a big inbox"): View offers Schedule, Conversations, Files, Programs and Your account, each with its unread count, read from the notice's `kind` by `NOTICE_TYPES` in `lib/notifications.ts` (no column; a kind or a prefix per type). From lists every team the person is on, always (from their memberships, so the page shows its order before anything arrives), plus any team they have notices from; Programs and Your account are types, not places. Each picker counts within the other's choice (`listNoticeFacets`); Mark all as read marks only what both choices show. Each row reads "Hosting Team · Schedule". **A new notice kind must be added to `NOTICE_TYPES`** (the harness fails on a recorded kind with no type). When members can write to each other, their notices are one more type, Messages.
- **Needs you** gathers, from every team the person is on, the sessions in the next two weeks they could take (`listHubNeeds`), with the Scheduler's own take button.
- **The email choice** (My Profile → Notifications, people on a team): team news emailed each one (default, as before), once a day (`lib/notificationsSummary.ts`, `notifications-daily-summary`, 13:30 UTC), or not at all. Emails about the person always come, and so does **urgent team news**: a session within two days that needs someone, or a cover request for one (`notice.urgent`, Jesse, 2026-10-20; a session still ahead, within 48 hours; the Monday list counts when it holds one). A coordinator's switch outranks urgency: a member whose team emails are off gets even urgent news only in Notifications. A team notice is "settled" when emailed or not wanted (`summarizedAt`); the summary carries only unsettled, unread team notices from the last week, and is marked only once sent. Changing the choice settles what's already there.
