# Hosting a Session on Zoom — A Quick Guide for Hosts

Hosting is simple, and you don't need a Zoom account or any setup. You start from
your RIM dashboard — the same place you sign in — and RIM walks you right into
Zoom. Here's everything you need.

## The short version

1. **Sign in to RIM** and open your **dashboard**.
2. Find today's session and click **Enter as host** (or **Join**).
3. On the next screen, note your **code**, then click **Join as host**.
4. In Zoom, open **Participants → Claim Host**, and type the **code**.
5. You're hosting. 🌿

Everything below is just those same five steps with a little more detail.

## Step by step

**1. Sign in and open your dashboard.**
Go to the RIM website and sign in the way you always do (your email and the
6‑digit code). You'll land on your dashboard — your home page.

**2. Find your session.**
Under **Today**, you'll see the session you're hosting. You can open the room
**early — about 30 minutes before it starts** — to settle in and welcome people.
Click **Enter as host** (the button may simply say **Join** once it's session
time). Either one takes you to the same place.

**3. The "You're hosting" screen.**
You'll see a calm little screen that says **"You're hosting today,"** the name of
the session, a **Join as host** button, and a **6‑digit code**. Keep that code
handy — it's how you'll pick up your host tools in a moment.

**4. Join — Zoom opens.**
Click **Join as host**. Zoom opens (in the Zoom app, if you have it installed —
most people do), and you're in the meeting **under your own name**. No Zoom
account or login needed.

**5. Claim your host controls.**
To get the host tools (mute people, manage the room, recording, ending the
session):

- In Zoom, open **Participants** (in the toolbar).
- Choose **Claim Host** — it may be tucked under **More** or **…**.
- Enter the **6‑digit code** from step 3.

That's it — you're the host. 🌿

## If you're the *backup* host

Sometimes you'll join a session you weren't assigned to lead. You'll see who
**today's host** is, along with the same code. If they're there, just join and
relax — there's nothing you need to do. If the main host doesn't show up, you can
**claim host with the code** and carry on, so the session is never left without a
host.

## A few good things to know

- **No Zoom account needed.** RIM brings you in by name.
- **Your name** appears as your first name and last initial (like "Maria L.").
- **Members never see the code.** They just click **Join** and go straight in —
  the code is only ever shown to hosts.
- **Only one host at a time.** If two of you are helping, one person claims host
  and can make the other a co‑host right inside Zoom.
- **If the session is set to record,** everyone sees Zoom's small recording
  indicator. That's normal and expected.

## If something's not working

- If Zoom doesn't open on its own, there's a **"click here to join"** link right
  on the screen — tap that.
- If you don't see **Claim Host**, someone may have already claimed it (there's
  only one host at a time).
- Anything else — reach out to Jesse or **support@rootedinmindfulness.org**, and
  we'll help you get in.
