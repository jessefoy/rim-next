# RIM Program Manager

**Status:** Active per-tool engineering reference. Created session 162 (2026-07-13).

The Program Manager is the operational application for creating and maintaining RIM's live offerings. Read this with `RIM_Offering_Model.md`, `RIM_Registration.md`, `RIM_Zoom.md`, and `RIM_Editor_Types.md`. Program changes are never isolated editor changes: a saved program can affect public promotion, registration, dana, dashboard placement, Zoom meetings, teaching, and volunteer coverage.

---

## Reminders counted from the start (Phase 4 slice 1, 2026-10-24)

What we tell them sets **The reminder**: *Days before it begins* (1 to 60; a new program starts at 2), *On a set date* (the older `reminderDate`, exactly as before), or *No reminder*. Choosing one clears the other. Days before (`Program.reminderDaysBefore`) is counted in Central calendar days from the first session and sent in the daily morning run (14:00 UTC: 8 AM Central in winter, 9 AM in summer), then each morning to anyone with a place who hasn't had it, until the program begins, so late registrants get it too. A program with several sessions is reminded once, before the first. When both are set, days before wins. Archived programs and programs with registration off get none. One rule: `lib/reminderSchedule.ts`, read by the cron (`app/api/cron/send-reminders`), the Registration desk's Going out soon and Needs you (no "registered after the reminder" for a program that still reaches them), and its program page. The harness checks it across the November time change.

## Who's asking (Phase 3 slice 2, 2026-10-24)

Each registration question has **Asked by** under it in How people join: the registration team (the default), the teacher (when one is linked; "The teacher, Jesse Foy", or "The teachers"), or one of the supporting teams ticked under Who's helping that staffs the format ("The Greeter Team"). Stored in the question as `askedBy`: absent for the registration team, `"teacher"`, or `"team:<hubId>"`, the team's id, so renaming a team never detaches its questions. The rules are `lib/questionAskers.ts`: the save routes keep a teacher or a team the program has after the save and drop anything else (`withAskers`); readers fall back to the registration team when no teacher is linked or the team is no longer on the program (`askerOf`), so unticking or archiving a team gives its questions to the registration team without a write. The registration team reads every answer. The public form and the update page group the questions under who is asking ("For the Greeter Team", "From Jesse Foy, your teacher", "For the registration team", each with "Read by … and the registration team.") only when someone other than the registration team asks; otherwise they look as before (`lib/programAskers.ts` loads the teachers and active supporting teams). **Teams that prepare** (Phase 3 slice 5): under Who's helping, a list of teams with the Helping with app installed (plus any the program already has); tick one, and optionally "Can reach people: sees their email and phone". Saved as `helperTeams` (`program_helpers`, by team id; a newly added team must be active with the app, `lib/programHelpers.ts`); safe saves compare it like any other field. A team that prepares can ask questions too ("The Kitchen Team" in Asked by), and taking the team off hands its questions to the registration team. The team sees the program in Helping with (`RIM_Hub_Engineering.md`, "Helping with"). **Who may choose (2026-10-24, from the slice 5 review):** only the Registrar, Admin and Guiding Teacher roles (`canUseRegistration`) may change who asks a question or which teams prepare and whether they can reach people, because that decides who sees people's answers and details. Both save routes refuse it otherwise (`askersChanged` in `lib/questionAskers.ts`; a `helperTeams` change), before anything is written; the editor locks Asked by and Teams that prepare for others with a line saying the registration team chooses them. Before this, a Programs editor without a role (a Program Central member) could give a team they're on everyone's email and phone. Wording provisional until Jesse's read-aloud.

## Opens on, Post a notice, Ready to be seen (Phase 2, 2026-10-24)

**Registration is one menu** in How people join: **Off · Not open yet · Open · Closed**, replacing the Registration enabled and Registration closed checkboxes. It stores what it always did (`registrationEnabled`, `registrationClosed`) plus **`Program.registrationOpensAt`** (nullable; Central in the editor, UTC stored, `centralToUtc`). "Not open yet" is registration on with an opening date still ahead; choosing it shows an **Opens on** date and time, and Save refuses it without a date still ahead ("Under How people join: …"). Open clears the opening date. Who can get into a session never depends on it: the Zoom door, My Home, Open entry and the mailing list's default read `registrationEnabled` and `openEntry`, never the opening date.

**One rule decides whether a program is taking sign-ups:** `lib/registrationWindow.ts` (`registrationWindow` → off, closed, notYet, open; `takingRegistrationsWhere` for queries; `fmtOpensAt`). Off first, then closed by hand, then a passed closing date, then an opening date ahead. The program page ("Registration opens on Sunday, November 1 at 9:00 AM."), the register page (the same, with no form; someone finishing their dana is never turned away), `POST /api/registrations` (403 before the date), course access's "registering now", Programs' team Home line, the Programs list (badge "Opens Nov 1, 2026"; its Open filter now also leaves out a program past its closing date, which it used to count), the Registration desk's state line and Registration's settings all read it. The harness checks it, across the November time change. Staff can still add a person before the opening date.

**Post a notice** sits at the top of the editor once a program is saved (`program-editor/NoticePanel.tsx`): the notice, Show until, and for the Registrar, Admin and Guiding Teacher roles "Also email the N people registered". It saves apart from Save, through `POST /api/programs-pg/[slug]/notice` (`canUsePrograms`; the email `canUseRegistration`, to everyone with a place, not the waiting list, one email per address, capped at 50, one at a time in `after()`; template `program-notice`, a notice in each person's Notifications under Programs; History line "posted the notice …"). An empty notice takes it down. The Notice and Show until fields left What we tell them, and the editor's Save no longer holds or sends them, so a save can never undo a notice posted meanwhile. When a notice is up, the button reads "Change the notice" and the notice shows beside it.

**Ready to be seen** (`program-editor/ReadyPanel.tsx`) is a short list above the sections: a check for what's set, a mark only for what keeps the program from being seen or joined (no description, no start date, no way to join, "Not open yet" without a date, no Category, a hidden old category, a passed date), and a dash for a choice (registration closed, hidden, Private, archived). Each line's name goes to its section. It's open when something needs a look and folded when not. It reads the same rules as the readouts: `listingReasonOf` in `program-editor/shared.tsx` is now the one source for Where it appears and this list. Wording provisional until Jesse's read-aloud.

## One page in sections (Phase 2, 2026-10-24)

The editor is one page in seven sections, in the order a person decides them: **About** (name, slug, tagline, image, description, pull quote and source, program notes); **When and where** (format, venue and location, start and end, recurrence, the schedule and time labels, the session note, and for virtual and hybrid programs Where it meets online with its "Who can join" readout, Recording and Open Access); **Who's helping** (teachers, Host coverage and No host needed, the hosting team, auxiliary role coverage); **How people join** (the visitors readout, Registration enabled, closed, Open entry, capacity, deadline, custom questions); **What we tell them** (confirmation message, reminder date and message, the notice and Show until, until Post a notice is built); **Dana**; **Where it appears** (Category, Format, Silent meditation, Hosted by volunteers, the old category, the "Where this program appears" readout, sort order, the hide options). The mockup is `mockups/registration-2026-10-23/index.html`.

- **Each section is its own memoized component** in `components/registrar/program-editor/` (`AboutSection`, `WhenWhereSection`, `HelpingSection`, `JoinSection`, `TellSection`, `DanaSection`, `AppearsSection`; `shared.tsx` holds the types, `DateTimePicker`, `EditorSection` and `SECTIONS`). `ProgramEditor.tsx` holds the program's state, the save, the section list and the guard. Pass a section only values and `useState` setters (stable) and the stable `markDirty`; an inline function or object prop would redraw it on every keystroke anywhere on the page (closes backlog `2026-06-25-002`). Logic that belongs to one section (teacher search, image upload, the question list, the guest-link reset, the dana templates) lives in it.
- **Finding your way:** on wide screens a list of the sections sits beside the form (`.pe-index`, sticky, the current section marked with `aria-current="location"`: the last section whose top has passed a line 220px from the top of the screen, worked out on scroll; asking which sections were in view marked the one above after a jump, since its end still showed); at 1100px and below a **Jump to** menu above the first section (`.pe-jump`), which follows the same rule. Choosing from the list scrolls to the section and moves focus to its heading; choosing from Jump to scrolls and leaves focus on the menu, so the keyboard can keep stepping through it. Neither marks the page unsaved (`data-pe-nav`). On phones Save and Cancel sit side by side in the sticky bar (74px tall; the stacked bar shared with the Course editor took 154px).
- **A refused save goes to its section:** a quote source without its quote to About, a missing Category or Format to Where it appears, an outside link problem to When and where; the problem is said beside Save as before.
- **Unsaved changes:** any change in the editor marks it unsaved (`onChangeCapture`/`onInputCapture`), and adding, removing or moving a question now does too; leaving asks first, as before. The Save bar stays at the bottom of the screen (`.pe-actions`, sticky).
- **The mailing list left the editor** (Jesse, 2026-10-24): Flodesk on/off and the segment are set in the Registration app's settings, where they matter (`RIM_Registration_App.md`). The editor neither shows nor sends `flodeskSync` or `flodeskSegmentId`; a new program starts Automatic. `ProgramData` keeps the two fields for `lib/programEditorData.ts`.
- Wording that named tabs now names sections (the outside-room and readout instructions, the Scheduler's "no programs" lines, the old categories page). Field labels are unchanged; renaming them waits for Jesse's read-aloud.

## Safe saves (Phase 2, 2026-10-24)

The editor sends only the fields that changed since the page opened (or since its last save), each with `_base`: its value then, in the editor's own terms. `PUT /api/programs-pg/[slug]` compares each against the program as stored now, through the same mapping the edit page uses (`lib/programEditorData.ts`: `programToEditorData`, `editorFieldValue`; teachers and supporting teams included). If anyone changed one of those same fields meanwhile (another editor, or the Registration app's settings), nothing is saved; the 409 names the fields in plain words (`EDITOR_FIELD_LABELS`) and the editor keeps its edits. Changes to other fields meanwhile are kept, since this save doesn't carry them. The response's `editorData` becomes the next save's starting point. Saving with nothing changed says Saved and sends nothing. Creating a program still sends everything. The route still accepts a full payload without `_base` (nothing else sends one).

## Editor structure

`components/registrar/ProgramEditor.tsx` is one page in seven sections (see "One page in sections" above; until 2026-10-24 it was seven tabs: Content, Schedule, Hosting & Access, Categories, Registration, Dana, Visibility). The notes below name the old tabs where they record history; today each field sits in the section listed above. It uses the shared `pe-` editor grammar. Rich authored fields follow the placements in `RIM_Editor_Types.md`; structured schedule, access, category, dana, and registration values remain form data.

**Category, Format, and Open entry (2026-10-02).** Every program carries a required **Category** (Foundations, Ongoing Learning & Practice, Immersion, Community Group, Special Event; and the internal Service and Private), a **Format** (Drop-in, Class, Course, Workshop, Day of mindfulness, Retreat; required for the three ways, optional otherwise), a **Silent meditation** checkbox (shown only when Format is Drop-in) and a **Hosted by volunteers** checkbox, all on the Categories tab with helper lines. Validation is `cleanOffering` in `lib/programOffering.ts`, shared by the editor and both API routes (`POST /api/programs-pg`, `PUT /api/programs-pg/[slug]`: 422 in the editor's voice; a PUT that omits the offering fields leaves them as stored, or takes them from the old category's meaning on a row never migrated). **Category and Format are labels and grouping only.** Access is the separate **Open entry** setting on the Registration tab, beside Registration enabled and Registration closed (`Program.openEntry`; POST default off; a PUT that omits it leaves it as stored; a non-boolean is 422). Open entry means anyone signed in may join, in person or on Zoom, without registering; it drives the program page's "Simply arrive" line, My Home's Today, Zoom entry, and This Week's Drop-in mark through `hasOpenEntry`. The old category stays visible as a read-only line on the Categories tab; the admin list filters by Category and by Format (its old Format column is now "Delivery"). `.pe-visibility-option` checkboxes are 44px tall.

**The pull quote is optional (2026-10-02; it was required from session 162).** A Program may be saved with no pull quote and no source. A source cannot stand without its quote: ProgramEditor blocks that save, and both API routes (`POST /api/programs-pg`, `PUT /api/programs-pg/[slug]`) return `422`; the PUT resolves each field against the stored value when the request omits it, and stores an empty value as `null`. A quote without a source is allowed (three live programs have exactly that). The public template handles a quote-less program with `pg-hero--no-quote` and `pg-content--no-quote` (see `RIM_Public_Pages.md`); the quote card is rendered only when `Program.pullQuote` is set. Program notes must be included in both create and update payloads.

**Dana texts and the dana templates (2026-10-01).** The Dana tab's `DANA_BUILTIN` list holds only starting templates; each program stores its own `danaMessage` (and a short `danaText` shown on the program page), and a template is copied in, not linked. The registration dana step and the program page now state where program dana goes (shared equally between RIM and the Teaching Fund, with a "How dana works at RIM" link to `/donate#dana-at-rim`), so the templates do not repeat it: **"General support"** is two sentences and **"Reciprocity"** (formerly "Teacher support") speaks only of the practice. Do not put the split, or any claim about which fund a gift goes to, in a template or a saved message. Saved messages that predate this (the older long Good Morning and Good Evening text, now replaced; the "General support" text on archived programs) are Jesse's to edit per record. `ProgramEditor.tsx` carries 7 lint errors that predate this work.

**Dated events retire themselves (session 172).** `Program.hideWhenPast` (default true) means "this one-time program retires itself": once its CT day has fully passed it leaves the public listings at read time (`hasConcludedOneTime` in `lib/programUtils.ts` — shared by `/community-programs` and the KM groups page) and the daily `archive-concluded-programs` cron sets `archivedAt` the next morning. The Visibility-tab checkbox renders **only for one-time programs** (`!recurrenceFreq`) — a recurring schedule never "passes"; the stored value persists invisibly if a program later gains recurrence, and is harmless because the concluded check is false for recurring. The Archived tab in `ProgramsTableClient` sorts by `archivedAt` desc (most recently archived first). Archiving is reversible and never touches registration records, which remain available to registrar operations and reporting. (The tab strip's `tablist` semantics, session 172, went with the tabs; the section list is a `nav` with `aria-current`.)

**"Where this program appears" (2026-09-24).** The Visibility tab opens with a derived, read-only readout (`.pe-readout`) stating whether the program is on the public Programs & Events page and on This Week, and naming the one reason when it isn't: archived, "Hide from public Programs & Events page" checked (which also hides it from This Week), no category, a hidden category (`ProgramCategory.hideFromProgramsPage`, passed to the editor as `Category.hidden`), a passed one-time date, the weekly box checked, or no start date. A third line covers **Member home** (the dashboard's Today list): archived, "Hide from member home" (until its optional auto-show date), no start date, in-person (registrants only), open entry (every member, with Join), or registration-required (registrants, not the waitlist, plus hosts and teachers). It mirrors the queries in `app/community-programs/page.tsx`, `app/this-week/page.tsx` and `app/account/(authenticated)/dashboard/page.tsx`; **change the readout whenever those rules change.** The Registration tab's "How this appears to visitors" uses the same class. The old hardcoded `slug: { not: "dummy-test-program" }` exclusions on the listing and home page were removed so the checkbox is the only control.

**Mailing list (Flodesk), 2026-10-12; moved to the Registration app 2026-10-24.** *History: until 2026-10-24* the end of the Registration tab held a three-way select, **Automatic / On / Off** (`Program.flodeskSync`: null, true, false), a readout of what will happen, and a segment picker. Automatic follows `programUsesFlodesk` in `lib/flodeskRules.ts` (on when registration is on, except Private and Service), the same function the server acts on. The readout names the linked segment, or the name a new one will take at the first registration (`programSegmentName`: "Name MM/DD/YY", Jesse's existing Flodesk naming; an ongoing program, name only). "Use an existing segment instead" loads `GET /api/flodesk/segments` (`canUsePrograms`, read-only) so a program already open keeps filling the segment it has. **The segment id is sent only when it was changed in the editor**, so a save can never erase a segment that a first registration created while the editor was open; the PUT verifies the id with Flodesk and stores Flodesk's name, never the request's. There is no "unlink": choosing "Create a new segment" is offered only while nothing is linked, because Flodesk cannot rename or delete segments and a fresh one would duplicate it. Mechanics and consent rules: `lib/flodesk.ts` and `RIM_Registration.md`.

The visitor-facing registration readout is intentionally present in the Registration tab. It translates registration state plus the **Open entry** setting (and the program's delivery) into the public consequence before a coordinator saves, with an "Open entry: on/off" line that must agree with the Visibility tab's Member home line. Preserve this clear-seeing bridge whenever those rules change.

**Hosting & Access and the schedule (2026-10-15/16).** The **Hosting team** list shows only teams that run sessions, have the Scheduler installed and staff the program's format (`hostingTeamRefusal` in `lib/programHub.ts`, also enforced by both routes before anything is written; the program's current team stays listed). **Auxiliary role coverage** lists supporting teams that staff the format; the server applies the same rule (`supportingTeamRefusal`) to newly ticked teams before writing, and the editor sends only ticked teams that still fit. **A hosting-team change clears the old team's upcoming sessions and rotations** (Jesse's ruling; the warning says so), and the people on them are told. **Saving a schedule change** (start date, days, frequency, count) re-times upcoming sessions to the new start and clears those on days the program no longer meets, with rotations that can't match; a note beside Save says what was cleared. **The teacher pill label** was retired 2026-10-16 (it did nothing after the move to Zoom; `Program.teacherLabel` stays, unused). Deleting a program removes its schedule rows.

## Full ecosystem trace

Before changing a Program field or save route, check every affected surface:

- public detail and schedule: `/programs/[slug]`, `/this-week`
- registration, dana, Stripe, waitlist, confirmation email
- member dashboard and public `/programs/[slug]`
- teachers and facilitator display
- Zoom provisioning and occurrence meeting teardown/self-heal
- Scheduler, hosting hub, auxiliary coverage hubs, and standing assignments
- recurrence helpers, calendar export, reminder jobs, and cached labels

Program slugs are join keys for host assignments. Treat an established slug as permanent.

## Authenticated design contract

The Program Manager is a compact work interface, not a public editorial page:

- render beneath the shared member header and inside `WorkspaceShell`
- use the `pe-` grammar and the tokens in `custom.css`; do not add a second page shell
- use the compact authenticated type scale; rich preview/content remains editorial
- use white working surfaces on the warm ground, with spacing and ground changes before borders or shadows
- the sections are one editor, each a white card under its own heading, with the section list (or Jump to) for finding your way; keep the save action and unsaved-change warning dependable
- align controls vertically within rows; “balanced” does not mean center-aligning labels or form content
- preserve 44px touch targets and 16px minimum mobile input text

## Common pitfalls

- Changing only the editor without tracing dashboard/public/registration/Zoom/Scheduler behavior.
- Deriving access from Category, Format, or the old `ProgramCategory.kind`. Access is `Program.openEntry` (null falls back to the old kind rule until the migration verifies); Category and Format are labels.
- Treating `endDatetime` as a recurring-series cutoff rather than the occurrence end time.
- Changing schedule fields without future Zoom meeting teardown and conflict evaluation.
- Gating a Programs page and the API behind it with different rules (a second rule is how the page and its saves drifted apart in 2026-10): editing is `canUsePrograms` on both; archive, delete, the guest-link reset and categories are `canUseProgramManager` on both.
- Spreading Prisma results containing `Date` values into client props; serialize explicitly.
- Adding a new rich field without registering its editor type and output placement.

## Verification

Use `npx tsc --noEmit` before pushing. The full build runs only on Vercel because the local build's migration stage cannot reach production. For a behavior change, verify the editor consequence and at least the affected public/member/operational surfaces—not merely a successful save.

### The two notices, named for their purpose (2026-10-09; renamed the same evening)

Jesse asked what the two fields were for and whether they could be optimized, then showed the old site's member page, where the same kind of line sat under the schedule as one quiet italic sentence ("Held in Noble Silence before and during session."): *"maybe the problem is how it's named and how it's displayed. A little too obviously or boldly and maybe 'update' and 'good to know' are a little funky."* So:

- **Notice** (`specialAnnouncement`) is a change: "Cancelled this week." "Starting at 7:15 tonight." "We are in the community room." The "Home Card" tab is gone, and Program Notes' help no longer invites "what to bring", so each field has one job (Program Notes for context and accessibility; Session note for the line on the day; Confirmation Message for the registrant's email).
