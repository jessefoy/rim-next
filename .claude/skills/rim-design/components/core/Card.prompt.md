White card on the Pampas ground — for one bounded thing a person can read or act on.

```jsx
<Card padding={32}><h3>A bounded piece of work</h3><p>…</p></Card>
```

Radii in the wild: 10px (default), 12px (list/program cards), 16px (pull-quote and details cards). Elevation is always `--card-shadow` — a whisper; never a heavier shadow.
