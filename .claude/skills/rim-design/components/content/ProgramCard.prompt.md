Editorial catalog card — one program per row in the programs listing.

```jsx
<ProgramCard title="Foundations of Mindfulness" tags={["Course"]} meta={["8 weeks", "Wednesdays", "6:30 PM"]} description="Where most people begin." />
```

Serif title, uppercase tags in `--rim-mid`, middot-separated meta. Hover lifts the fill to `--rim-bg-bright`, never the shadow.
