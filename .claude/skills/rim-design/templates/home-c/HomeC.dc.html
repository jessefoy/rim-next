Awaken your Mind, Open your Heart, Nourish your Life, Beautify the World.

A meditation and Dharma community in Brookfield, Wisconsin. Buddhist-rooted, open to everyone, offered in the spirit of generosity.

Scroll

People come to RIM for different reasons. Some want to learn to meditate. Some have been practicing alone and are ready for a teacher and a community. Some are going through something hard and need a place where they don't have to explain themselves.

Whatever brought you here, you're welcome. No experience needed. No fees. Come as you are, and see what you find.

If you are new

01

Choose a Tuesday or a Saturday.

Both drop-ins meet at 9:30 in the morning, in person at the center and online. They are the two easiest doors in, and there is nothing to register for.

02

Arrive as you are.

Wear whatever is comfortable. Sit in a chair or on a cushion, whichever your body prefers. Come a few minutes early and someone will show you where things are.

03

Stay for an hour.

Guided meditation, a short teaching, and time for questions. Stay for tea afterward if you'd like. There is no fee, and there never will be.

We practice for ourselves, for the people we love, and for our shared world.

What changes in you moves outward. The way you listen, the way you respond, the patience you bring home. The world needs people who are present, compassionate, and willing to show up.

Dana

Everything here is offered in the spirit of generosity of heart, mind, and action.

Traditionally, Buddhist nuns and monks offer teachings freely, while the community supports the teachers and the center to the level of their ability. RIM follows that model. We do not charge fees or tuition. This community is a living gift, made possible by the goodwill of the people who practice here.

RIM is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization.
