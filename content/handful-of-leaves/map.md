## 2. The Path as Refuge

1. Buddha — *Buddha* (Skt: Buddha)
2. Dharma — *Dhamma* (Skt: Dharma)
3. Sangha — *Saṅgha* (Skt: Saṃgha)

**Wisdom — *Paññā***

1. Right View — *Sammā-diṭṭhi* (Skt: Samyag-dṛṣṭi)
2. Right Intention — *Sammā-saṅkappa* (Skt: Samyak-saṃkalpa)

**Ethics — *Sīla***

3. Right Speech — *Sammā-vācā* (Skt: Samyag-vāc)
4. Right Action — *Sammā-kammanta* (Skt: Samyak-karmānta)
5. Right Livelihood — *Sammā-ājīva* (Skt: Samyag-ājīva)

**Cultivation — *Samādhi***

6. Right Effort — *Sammā-vāyāma* (Skt: Samyag-vyāyāma)
7. Right Mindfulness — *Sammā-sati* (Skt: Samyak-smṛti)
