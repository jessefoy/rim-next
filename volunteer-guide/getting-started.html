Getting started with the RIM Guide

A helper that knows how RIM's new website works, and why we built it the way we did.

What you can do with it

  • Ask how to do something. "How do I mark that someone paid by check?"
  • Ask what a page is for, or why something works the way it does.
  • Tell it what you notice, in your own words. It helps you turn it into a short note for Jesse.

Good to know

  • You can't break anything. It can look, but it can't change the website at all.
  • It sees what you see. You sign in with your RIM account, so it shows you only what your account can open on RIM.
  • Your chats are private. Jesse only sees what you send him.
  • It's always up to date. It reads the guide from the website each time.

1. Join RIM's Claude Once

  1. Look in your RIM email for a message from Claude inviting you to join Rooted in Mindfulness. Open it and press the button to accept.
  2. On Claude's sign-in page, choose Continue with Google and pick your RIM account. Or type your RIM email address and press Continue with email: Claude emails you a code to type in.
  3. Always sign in with your RIM email. That's how Claude knows you're part of RIM.

2. Connect the RIM Guide Once

  1. In the menu on the left side of Claude, choose Customize, then Connectors.
  2. Find RIM Guide in the list and press Connect.
  3. RIM's sign-in page opens. Sign in the way you do on RIM's website: type your email, then the 6-digit code RIM emails you. (If you're already signed in to RIM, this step is skipped.)
  4. On the page that says Connect the RIM Guide, press Allow. The guide can now see what you see on RIM. It can't change anything.

3. Open the RIM Guide Each time

  1. Go to claude.ai on your computer, or open the Claude app on your phone.
  2. In the menu on the left, choose Projects, then RIM Guide. (If you don't see it, look under Shared with me.)
  3. Type your question in the box and press Enter. Ask it the way you'd ask a person.

Important: always start inside the RIM Guide project

A chat started anywhere else in Claude is an ordinary chat that doesn't know about RIM. If you aren't sure where you are, go to Projects → RIM Guide and start again from there.

4. Send Jesse a note

When you tell the guide something you'd change, or something that seemed off, it asks a few questions, one at a time. Then it writes a short note in a gray box, like this:

RIM FEEDBACK From: LoriLee · Tue Oct 27, 2026 Kind: Confusing Where: Programs list, a program's row Trying to do: see who registered for a program What happened: clicking the program's name opened the editor Expected: the list of people In their words: "I clicked on the program and it didn't show me the people"
  1. Press the copy button at the top right corner of the gray box.
  2. Start a new email to jesse@rootedinmindfulness.org and paste the note into it.
  3. If you took a screenshot, attach it. Then send.

One note for each thing. If you mention several, the guide makes a note for each.

Things to try first

  • "What can you help me with?"
  • "What's the difference between Programs and Registration?"
  • "How do I add someone who signed up by phone?"
  • "Why doesn't my new program show on the website yet?"
  • "Read me every hint in the program editor's Dana section."

If something isn't working

  • Claude asks if it may use the RIM Guide. Choose to allow it. If there's an Always allow choice, pick that so it won't ask again.
  • It asks you to sign in again. Go back to step 2 and press Connect. Sign in and press Allow.
  • It says it can't reach the guide. In the chat box, press the + button at the lower left, then Connectors, and make sure RIM Guide is turned on. If it's missing, go back to step 2.
  • You don't see the RIM Guide project. Check that you signed in with your RIM email, then ask Jesse.
  • It doesn't know the answer. It's meant to say so instead of guessing. That's a good question to ask Jesse directly.

Questions about the guide itself? Ask Jesse. Something personal, or a concern about someone in the community? Please reach Jesse directly rather than through the guide.
