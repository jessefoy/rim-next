# RIM Guide: Site Map

This file lists every page and app a volunteer can land on at RIM's website. Use it to work out which page someone means when they say "the page with the list" or paste a screenshot. Match what they describe against the headings, buttons and filters named below.

The website lives at **rim-next.vercel.app** until it moves to rootedinmindfulness.org. Every address below starts with that. The old site at rootedinmindfulness.org is a different website; if someone describes a page that isn't here, ask whether they were on the old site.

All times on the site are Central Time.

---

## How to find out which page someone is on

Ask, in this order:

1. **What is the big title near the top of the page?** Most pages have one heading in large serif letters (for example "Programs", "Registration", "My Home", "Notifications").
2. **What is in the list down the left side?** Signed-in pages have a list of links on the left (the "rail"). If it starts with **My Home**, they are on a personal page. If it starts with a team's name and **Home**, they are in a team's space. If there is no left list and a slim bar under the header, they opened an app directly.
3. **What does the address bar say?** The part after the website name settles it (for example `/tools/registration`).
4. On a phone there is no left list. The site's **Menu** button (top right) opens a sheet that lists the same places.

## The site's frame (the parts on every page)

### The header

Every page, public or signed in, wears the same white header across the top. On the left is the RIM logo; clicking it always goes to the home page. On a wide screen the header shows these menus:

- **New to RIM** (a plain link)
- **Practice** (Why We Practice, Taking CARE, Our Roots, A Handful of Leaves)
- **Programs** (Foundations, Programs & Events, This Week's Schedule)
- **About** (About RIM, Diverse Together, Community Care Agreements)
- **Get Involved** (Volunteer, Community Groups, Outreach for Organizations)
- **Members** when signed out (Become a Member, Sign in). When signed in, this place shows a plain **My Home** link instead.
- **Donate** (a gold button)

On narrower screens (tablets and phones) the menus fold into a **Menu** button. The sheet it opens lists the same groups. When someone is signed in, the sheet also has a **My RIM** group at the top with their own pages (My Home, Notifications, Library, My Teams, Teaching, Community Care, My Profile, and Manage RIM for staff).

The header never changes when someone moves into their own pages. This is on purpose: many members are older and not comfortable with technology, and the header is how they know they are still at RIM.

### The footer

Public pages and personal pages end with a blue footer: a newsletter sign-up ("Stay Connected"), contact details, and links. Working pages (a team's space, the apps, staff administration) leave the footer out, so they end where the work ends.

### The left rail (signed-in pages)

There are two kinds of left list. Only one shows at a time.

**The personal rail ("My RIM")**, on a member's own pages:
- **My Home**
- **Notifications** (with a number when there are unread ones)
- **Library**
- **My Teams** (only for someone on a team, and the guiding teacher)
- **Teaching** (only for someone teaching a current program, and the guiding teacher)
- then **Community Care** and **My Profile**
- **Manage RIM** (only for staff who manage the member records)
- **Sign out** at the bottom

**The team rail**, inside a team's space:
- the team's name at the top
- **Home**
- the team's apps (for example **Scheduler**, **Registration**, **Program Manager**), right under Home
- **Updates** (with a number of items unread for you)
- **Conversations**
- **Files**
- **Members**
- **Trash** (for the team's leads only)
- **Team settings** (staff administrators only)
- at the foot: **My Teams** (the way back to the list of teams), **My Profile**, **Sign out**

On a computer, either rail can be narrowed to a column of small icons with the **Collapse navigation** button and widened again. The choice is remembered. On phones the rail becomes a drawer opened by a button at the top of the page.

### Ways back

Apps and editors carry a plain text link back at the top, with a left arrow, such as **← Programs** or **← All programs**. Pages that stop someone (the Zoom door, an ended program) offer a named way back such as **Back to My Home** or **Back to the program page**.

### How buttons look on working pages

On team pages, apps and administration there are three kinds of button:
- **Filled** (blue, or red for a delete that is being confirmed): the button that does the work, like **Save**, **Send**, **Add them**, or the "Yes" in a question.
- **Quiet text** with no box: buttons that open something or are secondary, like **People**, **Edit**, **Add a person**, **Message people**, **Cancel**.
- **Plain text with an arrow**: the way back.

A few lists whose whole purpose is one action keep that action filled, such as the Scheduler's take buttons and Updates' **Take this session**. The public pages and personal pages use the public site's own rounded buttons.

## Signing in and joining

There are no passwords. A person types their email, gets a 6-digit code by email, and types it in.

| Page | Address | What's on it |
|---|---|---|
| Sign in | `/login` | Title **Sign in**: an email box and a button to send the code. If the person typed an address they used to use, it says **Your sign-in email has changed** and offers to send the code to the current one. |
| Enter your code | `/login/check-email` | A box for the 6-digit code and **Sign in**. The code email also has a button, "Sign me in from this device", which fills the code in. |
| Sign-in problem | `/login/error` | **We could not sign you in**, with a way to try again. Usually the code was old or already used. |
| Become a member | `/join` | What membership means, the Community Care Agreements, and a short form ending in **Join the community →**. A member who is already signed in sees **You're already a member** instead. |
| Welcome | `/account/welcome` | The first time someone signs in (if they were added ahead of time), they read the agreements here and agree before going on. |
| Welcome back | `/account/reactivate` | For a member whose account was archived, to come back. |

## Personal pages (signed in; each person sees their own)

These pages keep the site's header and footer, with the personal rail on the left.

### My Home
- **Address:** `/account/dashboard`
- **Who:** every signed-in member.
- **For:** the member's day. What is happening today and how to join it.
- **On it:** the date and "Good morning, [first name]." (or afternoon, evening). A line when there are new notifications ("You have 3 new notifications"). A **Today** section: each session today with its time and its state (**Open now**, **Opens at …**, **Later today**, **Happening now**), and a **Join** button when the room is open. A program's notice can appear with a blue edge; a session note shows as a quiet italic line. Hosts see **Open early as host** or **Enter Zoom as host**. Someone on a supporting team that day sees a quiet line such as "You're greeting" with **Today's list →**. A teacher sees "You're teaching" with **Who's coming →**. Then **This week's schedule**. When nothing is left today: "There are no more sessions today."
- **Leads to:** the Zoom door (Join), the program's page, Today's list, the teacher's page, This Week.
- **Not on it:** upcoming registrations. That list was removed to be rethought.

### Notifications
- **Address:** `/account/notifications`
- **Who:** every signed-in member.
- **For:** one inbox for everything RIM has emailed the person about: their sessions, registrations, receipts, account, and their teams' news.
- **On it:** filter pills **All** and **Unread**, a **From** picker (Everywhere, or one team), and a **View** picker (Everything, Schedule, Conversations, Files, Programs, Your account), each with a count. A **Needs you** group shows sessions on their teams in the next two weeks that need someone. Each row is one line, such as "Hosting Team · Schedule". Tapping a row opens the details and one button named for where it goes (**Open the schedule**, **Open the conversation**, **Open the file**, **See the program**, **Open the team**). **Mark all as read** at the top.
- **Leads to:** wherever each notice points.

### Library
- **Address:** `/account/courses`
- **Who:** every signed-in member.
- **For:** a page in preparation. It says what the Library will hold and that members will hear when it opens. One button to This week's schedule.

### My Teams
- **Address:** `/account/teams`
- **Who:** people on a team, and the guiding teacher.
- **For:** the list of teams the person belongs to; the door into each team's space.
- **On it:** **Your teams**, one row per team, with **Coordinator** marked where they lead it. The guiding teacher also sees **Teams you oversee**. Someone with no team sees "You are not part of a team yet." and a way to volunteer.
- **Leads to:** a team's Home.

### Teaching
- **Addresses:** `/account/teaching` (the list) and `/account/teaching/[program]` (one program)
- **Who:** a program's teachers, and the guiding teacher. Staff administrators alone do not see it.
- **For:** the teacher's view of who is coming.
- **On it:** the list shows **Programs you teach** (and for the guiding teacher, **Programs you oversee**). One program's page says how many are coming, "You see this because you teach this program.", **First registration here** (people new to RIM's site), **What they told you** (answers to the teacher's own questions, and **Notes from the registration team** marked for the teacher), and **Everyone coming** (open a name for their email, phone and answers), then the people waiting. It never shows dana. 30 days after a program ends, its list closes to the teacher; until then the program is listed under **Ended**.

### Today's list
- **Address:** `/account/greeting/[program]` (with the date and team)
- **Who:** someone on a supporting team (such as greeters or audio/visual) who is on that session, that team's leads, and the guiding teacher.
- **For:** one session as the team sees it at the door.
- **On it:** the people with a place, their answers to that team's own questions, and an **Arrived** button beside each name. A line for walk-ins. No contact details, no dana. For a program people don't register for, it says to welcome everyone who comes. Opened without a date, it shows the next session.
- **Reached from:** My Home's duty line, **Today's list →**.

### Community Care
- **Address:** `/account/community-care`
- **Who:** every signed-in member.
- **For:** reading the agreements again. It shows the date the member agreed ("You agreed to hold them on …") and the "If harm happens" door.

### My Profile
- **Address:** `/account/dashboard-my-profile`
- **Who:** every signed-in member (their own).
- **On it:** sections **Personal details**, **Sign-in email**, **Photo and introduction**, **Household**, and, for people on a team, **Notifications** (how team news is emailed: each one, once a day, or not at all; emails about the person always come).

### A Handful of Leaves (members' pages)
- **Addresses:** `/account/handful-of-leaves` and `/account/handful-of-leaves/map`
- **Who:** signed-in members. Reading pages for members, with a print style. Not in the rail.

## A team's space (signed in; team members only)

Every team has the same space. The address starts with `/account/hub/[team]` (the word "hub" in addresses is the old name; people always read "team"). Who sees it: the team's members and the guiding teacher. Staff administrators configure teams but only enter a team's space as members.

### Home
- **Address:** `/account/hub/[team]`
- **On it:** the team's name and its own one-line description (written by its leads). One card for the team's main app (for example "Open Registration" or the Scheduler) with a one-line summary. Then quiet destinations: **Conversations** ("Read and join team discussions"), **Files** ("Find documents and shared resources"), **Members** ("Find the people on your team"), and any links the team uses.

### Updates
- **Address:** `/account/hub/[team]/activity`
- **For:** what has happened on the team, read like an inbox.
- **On it:** pills **All**, **For you**, **Unread** with counts; a **View** picker (Everything, Schedule, Conversations, Files, Members). **Needs you** at the top: the team's sessions in the next two weeks that need someone, with **Take this session**, a sign-up button, or **I'll cover this** right on the item. History under day headings (Today, Yesterday, then dates). **Mark all as read**. Every item opens the exact thing it is about.

### Conversations
- **Address:** `/account/hub/[team]/conversations` (one topic at `/conversations/[topic]`)
- **On it:** **New topic**, a category filter (**All categories**), **Active** and **Archived**, pinned topics at the top. A topic has replies; leads can **Pin to top**, **Close thread**, **Rename**, **Delete**. Empty state: "No conversations yet. Start a topic to get the conversation going."

### Files
- **Address:** `/account/hub/[team]/files`
- **For:** the team's Google Drive folder, shown inside RIM. Nobody needs a Google account; documents open in Google.
- **On it:** title **Files**; **New folder**, **New document**, **New spreadsheet**, **New presentation**, and upload; search ("Search file names"); **Sort** (Recently updated, Name A–Z and others) and **File type** filters; **Favorites**; **Your drafts** (files only you see until you share them). Leads also see **Team files** and **Leads only** and can move files between them. A member can **Request removal** of a file; a lead approves or keeps it ("Pending removal"). Each file also has its own page with comments.

### Members
- **Address:** `/account/hub/[team]/members`
- **On it:** the team's people, with **Coordinators** first. Leads see more: a search to add a member ("Search for a member to add"), each person's **Status** (Active, Paused, Inactive), **Can take sessions on the schedule**, **Receives team notifications**, private notes, and "Hasn't signed in yet. Team emails start when they do." Marking someone **Inactive** asks the lead to confirm ("Mark inactive and open their sessions"), then opens their sessions to the team. Pausing someone asks whether to keep their sessions or take them off.

### Trash
- **Address:** `/account/hub/[team]/trash`
- **Who:** the team's leads (and staff). Conversations that were deleted, with **Restore**.

## The apps (signed in; by role or team)

Apps open either from a team's rail (they keep the team's rail around them) or directly by address (a slim bar replaces the rail, with My Profile and Sign out).

### Programs (the Program Manager)
- **Addresses:** `/tools/programs` (the list), `/tools/programs/new` (start a program), `/tools/programs/[program]/edit` (the editor), `/tools/programs/categories` (categories)
- **Who:** the Registrar, Admin and Guiding Teacher roles, and active members of the Registration Team and Program Central. Archiving, restoring, deleting and the categories page stay with the three roles.
- **For:** setting programs up. It shows no one's personal details; people live in Registration.
- **The list:** title **Programs**, "Every program, with its category, format, delivery and registration." Pills **All**, **Open**, **Archived**, with counts; the registration roles also see **Has waitlist** and **Needs attention**. A search ("Search programs"), **All categories** and **All formats** filters. One row per program: the name, what needs a look, the kind, registration as **Off**, **Open**, **Closed** or **Opens …**, and places. Each row has a quiet **Edit**; the registration roles also see **People** and **Archive**, and only they see how many places are taken (others see the capacity, "20 places" or "No limit"). On phones the rows become short cards.
- **Starting a program:** title **What are you offering?** with six shapes to choose from, or copy an existing program (never its people, dates, address or notice).
- **The editor:** one long page with seven sections, listed beside the form on a computer (**Sections of this program**) or in a **Jump to** menu on phones: **About**, **When and where**, **Teachers and teams**, **How people join**, **Messages to those who register**, **Dana**, **Where it appears**. At the top: **Post a notice** (a one-line notice on the program, optionally emailed to the people registered) and **Ready to be seen** (a short checklist of what keeps the program from being seen or joined). A **Save** button stays in reach; after a save that changes the time, place or confirmation, it asks **Tell the people registered?** The way back is **← Programs**. The browser tab shows the program's name.

### Registration
- **Addresses:** `/tools/registration` (the desk), `/tools/registration/[program]` (one program), `/tools/registration/[program]/sign-in` (the printed sign-in list)
- **Who:** the Registrar, Admin and Guiding Teacher roles only. Being on a team never opens it. In the Registration Team's space it is the main app, and it is hidden from members without the role.
- **For:** the people side of every program: who's coming, what needs a person, dana, notes and history.
- **The desk:** title **Registration**, "What needs a person, then every program with registration on." Sections **Needs you**, **Going out soon** (reminders and follow-up notes due in the next two weeks, with their sending time), and **Programs with registration on**, each with **Open →**.
- **One program:** a state line such as "Open · 14 places · closes Nov 6, 2026 · mailing list on". **Needs you** (people waiting, dana not finished, people who haven't had the reminder), with the registration settings folded under it (Registration: Open, Not open yet, Closed; places; closing date; **Mailing list**; **Save settings**). **People**, with filters and a search ("Search people by name or email"), and a card for each person: **Their place**, **Dana**, **Their answers**, **Notes** (**Add note**), **Contact**, **History**. Quiet actions: **Add a person**, **Message people**, **Print a sign-in list**, **Download a spreadsheet**, **Offer them a place**, **Send a dana reminder**, **Send the reminder now**. A **History** tab lists what was done. The way back is **← All programs**.
- **Dana amounts** show only to the three roles. Anyone else helping with a program sees whether dana is finished, never the amount.

### Scheduler
- **Addresses:** `/tools/schedule` (opened with the team), `/tools/schedule/program/[program]` ("Program staffing")
- **Who:** members of a team that has the Scheduler, each for their own team; also the scheduling managers and Admin. Leads see more.
- **For:** who is covering each session: hosts for online sessions, greeters and audio/visual for in-person ones, guides for the peer-led sits.
- **On it:** tabs **Schedule** and, for leads, **Rotations**. Pills **All**, **My schedule**, **Needs help** (amber). A small month calendar with **Previous month** and **Next month**, and a list of sessions by date. Each session shows its time, program and who is on it, or a need such as **Needs a host**, **Greeter needed**, **AV needed**. Buttons in the team's words: **Sign up**, **Yes, I can host this**, **I'll be the first**, **Yes, I can cover AV**; **Ask the team to cover** when you can't make it; **Enter room →** for a host on the day. Leads also see **Assign someone…** and **Manage coverage**. Empty: "Everything is covered. Thank you, team."
- **Rotations** are the regular turns (for example the first Sunday of every month, or every other week).

### Course Manager
- **Address:** `/tools/learning` (series), `/tools/learning/lessons` (lessons)
- **Who:** people with the Teacher role, Admin, and members of a team that has it.
- **For:** online courses and their lessons. There are no courses offered yet.

### Helping with
- **Addresses:** `/tools/helping` and `/tools/helping/[program]`
- **Who:** active members of a team that prepares for programs (for example a kitchen team), and the guiding teacher.
- **For:** the programs this team prepares for: how many are coming and the answers to the team's own questions. Email and phone show only when the program's editor has ticked that this team can reach people. Never dana. Each program stays 30 days after it ends, under **Ended**.
- **On it:** title **Helping with**; empty: "No program to help with right now."

## The Zoom door

- **Address:** `/session/[program]/enter` (the older `/session/[program]` forwards here)
- **Who:** anyone who presses Join, from My Home, a program's page or This Week.
- **For:** RIM checks who is joining and whether it's time, then opens Zoom. Sessions run on Zoom; RIM decides who gets in.
- **On it:** usually just "Opening Zoom…" or "Connecting you", "The meeting should open automatically in a moment.", with **Open the meeting manually** if it doesn't. Hosts see **Join as host →** and the steps to claim host. When it stops someone it says why in a plain page: when the room opens (with the day and time), "This session is for registered participants", "This program has ended", or a sign-in. Each stop has a named way back (**Back to My Home**, **Back to the program page**, **See current programs**).
- A program can also meet in an outside room (for example another organization's Zoom or Google Meet). The door checks the same things, then forwards to that link; the button says which service ("Join on Google Meet").

## Tutorials

- **Addresses:** `/tutorials`, one tutorial at `/tutorials/[name]`, and a played version at `/tutorials/[name]/watch`
- **Who:** anyone with the link. Not in the menu and not in search engines.
- **On it:** eyebrow "Help with the website", title **Tutorials**. **Start here** for everyone, in order (Signing in, Finding your way, Your team's space, Emails and notifications). Then **Find your team**: Hosting Team, Peer-Led Silent Meditation, Greeter, Audio/Visual, Registration Team, each with its own tutorials, and **If you lead this team** for leads (Leading your team, The Scheduler for leads). The Registration Team's two are **Setting up a program** and **Looking after the people**.
- Each tutorial is a short series of steps, each with a drawing of the screen (not a screenshot), on phone or computer. **Next tutorial** at the end. Some have **Watch it**, which plays the steps like a video with captions.

## Staff administration ("Manage RIM")

- **Reached from:** **Manage RIM** in the personal rail or the phone menu.
- **Who:** the Registrar and Admin roles see **Members** and **Households**. Only Admin sees the rest.
- **Pages:**
  - **Members** (`/admin/members`): the canonical record of every person, with filters by role, team and status; each person's profile.
  - **Households** (`/admin/households`).
  - **Form submissions** (`/admin/form-submissions`): every volunteer form and Kalyana Mitta application.
  - **Teams** (`/admin/hubs`): create and configure teams, install apps.
  - **Emails** (`/admin/emails`): the wording of RIM's automatic emails.
  - **Google Files** (`/admin/google-files`) and connection test pages for Google and Zoom.
  - **Import members** (`/admin/import-legacy`): temporary, for bringing people over from the old site.
- Volunteers do their work in their team's space and the apps, never in the Member Registry. This is a deliberate boundary.

## Matching common descriptions

- "The page with my sessions for today" → **My Home**.
- "Where I see if anyone needs a sub" → the **Scheduler** (pill **Needs help**), or **Updates** and **Notifications** (**Needs you**).
- "The list of all the programs with Edit and People" → **Programs** (`/tools/programs`).
- "The long form with sections for a program" → the program **editor**.
- "Where I see who registered and their dana" → **Registration**, one program's page.
- "The list with Arrived buttons" → **Today's list**.
- "Who's coming to my class" (a teacher) → **Teaching**.
- "The page that says the room isn't open yet" → the **Zoom door**.
- "My messages" → ask whether they mean **Notifications** (their own inbox) or a team's **Conversations**.
- "The calendar" → usually the **Scheduler**; for the public, **This Week**.
- "The files" or "the documents" → the team's **Files**.
- "Settings" → ask which: a program's settings (Registration's settings or the editor), a team member's settings (**Members**, for leads), or their own (**My Profile**).

If a description still doesn't match, ask for the address bar or a screenshot. If it still doesn't match, it may be the old site at rootedinmindfulness.org, or something new. Not certain: ask Jesse.
