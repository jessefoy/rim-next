# RIM tutorials, word for word

The tutorials live at rim-next.vercel.app/tutorials. They are short guides with drawings of RIM's screens, one step at a time, for a phone or a computer. Anyone with the link can open them; they are not in the site's menus, so Jesse shares them by link. Each tutorial page has a switch, "On a phone" / "On a computer", that swaps the drawings and the few words that differ. The Signing in tutorial can also be watched like a short video, at its address followed by /watch.

The page is one path. Everyone begins with **Start here**, read in order. Then **Find your team**: each team's own tutorials, in order, and under "If you lead this team" the ones for its leads.

Below is the text of every tutorial as it appears on the site. Where a step's words point at something in its drawing, a short line in square brackets says what the drawing shows. The wording of the tutorials is still provisional until Jesse has read it aloud, except the Registration Team's two, which he has read and approved.

## Start here

For everyone, in this order.

### Signing in

Address: rim-next.vercel.app/tutorials/signing-in
Who it is for: For everyone
Part 1 of 4 of Start here.

Your email, the code, your first visit's Welcome page, and what to do if the code doesn't come.

**Step 1. Ask for a sign-in code**

On the RIM website, tap Menu, then Sign in. Type the email address RIM has for you and choose Send code. There's no password to remember.

Tip: **Which email?** The one RIM writes to you at. If the page says “We don't have an account for” that address, try your other email, or write to support. (Become a member is for people who are new to RIM.)

On a computer this step reads:
On the RIM website, choose Members at the top of the page, then Sign in. Type the email address RIM has for you and choose Send code. There's no password to remember.

**Step 2. Open the email with your code**

In a minute or two, an email arrives from Rooted In Mindfulness with a 6-digit code. If you're reading it on the same phone or computer, choose Sign me in from this device. Otherwise, note the six digits.

Tip: **Nothing yet?** Look in your spam or junk folder. The code works for 30 minutes.

**Step 3. Type the code and choose Sign in**

Type the six digits in the boxes and choose Sign in. (If you used the button in the email, the code is already filled in; just choose Sign in.)

**Step 4. The first time: Welcome**

The very first time, a Welcome page asks for your first and last name and shows our Community Care Agreements. Read them, tick the box, and choose Step back in (or Join the community, if you're new). Leave I'd rather not join alone: it removes your account.

Tip: **Your first sign-in matters.** It's what turns on your team's emails. Until you sign in once, your team can't reach you.

**Step 5. You're in: this is My Home**

You arrive at My Home, with the note “You're all set.” From now on, signing in brings you here.

Tip: **Next time** is the same: your email, then a code. You usually stay signed in on that phone or computer, so you won't need a code every time.

**Step 6. If the code doesn't come**

Below the code boxes, choose send a new code. If you typed the wrong address, choose Start over and type it again.

[Drawing: The Enter your code page with send a new code marked.]

Tip: **“We could not sign you in”?** The code was wrong or had run out, or there were several tries in a short time. Wait a few minutes, choose Try again, and ask for a new code.

**Step 7. Signing out**

On a shared phone or computer, sign out when you're done: open Menu and choose Sign out at the bottom.

On a computer this step reads:
On a shared computer, sign out when you're done: choose Sign out at the bottom of the column on the left.

**If you need help**

Ask your team's coordinators (they're marked Coordinator on your team's Members page), or write to support@rootedinmindfulness.org and someone will help you.

### Finding your way

Address: rim-next.vercel.app/tutorials/finding-your-way
Who it is for: For everyone
Part 2 of 4 of Start here.

My Home, your menu, and finding your teams.

**Step 1. My Home is your day**

My Home shows today's gatherings. On a day you're serving, your session shows here too. This week's schedule shows the whole week.

**Step 2. Your menu: My RIM**

Tap Menu at the top of the screen. Under My RIM are your own pages: My Home, Notifications, Library, My Teams, Community Care and My Profile.

Tip: **Lost?** While you're signed in, My Home is always the first thing in the Menu.

On a computer this step reads:
The column on the left holds your own pages, under My RIM: My Home, Notifications, Library, My Teams, Community Care and My Profile.

**Step 3. Open My Teams**

Choose My Teams to see the teams you're on.

Tip: **Don't see My Teams?** It appears once you're on a team. Ask your coordinator to add you.

**Step 4. Choose your team**

Each team you're on is listed. Choose one to go to its own space.

Tip: **Don't see your team?** Ask its coordinator to add you, or write to support.

**Step 5. Your team's home**

Your team's home shows its tool (the Scheduler) and three of its pages: Conversations, Files and Members.

**Step 6. Your team's pages**

Just under the top bar, beside your team's name, is ☰. Tap it to see all your team's pages: Home, Scheduler, Updates, Conversations, Files and Members, with My Teams at the bottom.

Tip: **Two menus:** Menu at the top right is for your own pages and the website. ☰ beside the team's name is for this team.

On a computer this step reads:
Your team's pages are in the column on the left: Home, Scheduler, Updates, Conversations, Files and Members.

**Step 7. Getting back**

Inside a team, tap ☰ and choose My Teams at the bottom to go back to your teams. For My Home, open Menu at the top right: it's first. The RIM logo goes to the website's home page.

On a computer this step reads:
Inside a team, My Teams is at the bottom of the column on the left; choose it to go back to your teams. My Home is always at the top right. The RIM logo goes to the website's home page.

**If you need help**

Ask your team's coordinators (they're marked Coordinator on your team's Members page), or write to support@rootedinmindfulness.org and someone will help you.

### Your team's space

Address: rim-next.vercel.app/tutorials/your-teams-space
Who it is for: For everyone on a team
Part 3 of 4 of Start here.

Updates and Needs you, Conversations, Files, and Members.

**Step 1. Updates: what's new**

Tap ☰ beside your team's name, then Updates. It's your team's news, newest first. Tap any line to open the exact thing: the conversation, the file, the session. Unread shows only what you haven't opened yet.

Tip: **Caught up?** Mark all as read clears the dots. Nothing leaves the list.

On a computer this step reads:
In the column on the left, choose Updates. It's your team's news, newest first. Choose any line to open the exact thing: the conversation, the file, the session. Unread shows only what you haven't opened yet.

**Step 2. Needs you**

At the top of Updates, Needs you lists the sessions in the next two weeks that still need someone. You can take one right there.

**Step 3. Conversations**

Conversations are your team's discussions, one topic each. Open Conversations from your team's home (or ☰), and tap a topic to read it.

On a computer this step reads:
Conversations are your team's discussions, one topic each. Choose Conversations in the column on the left, then a topic to read it.

**Step 4. Reply to a conversation**

At the bottom of a topic, write in the box and choose Post reply. Everyone following the topic is told.

Tip: **Follow** beside the title tells you about every reply (by email, as you choose in My Profile). It changes to Following; tap it again to stop.

**Step 5. Start a new topic**

Choose + New topic, give it a title and write your message, then choose Post topic.

Tip: **Who hears?** Your team's coordinators are told automatically. Under Notify team members you can tick anyone else who should see it.

**Step 6. Files**

Files holds your team's shared documents. Tap one to open it right here.

Tip: **Looking for something?** Type part of its name in Search file names.

**Step 7. A file's page**

A document opens on its own page. To change it, choose Edit in Google Docs; it opens in Google, and you don't need a Google account. Below it, you can leave a comment for the team. Remove doesn't delete anything at once: it asks a team lead.

[Drawing: A file's page with Edit in Google Docs marked.]

Tip: **In Google,** your edits may show as “Anonymous” with an animal's name. That's normal. On a phone, Google may offer its Docs app; either works.

**Step 8. Members**

Members lists everyone on your team. The Coordinators at the top are your team's leads: the people to ask.

**If you need help**

Ask your team's coordinators (they're marked Coordinator on your team's Members page), or write to support@rootedinmindfulness.org and someone will help you.

### Emails and notifications

Address: rim-next.vercel.app/tutorials/emails-and-notifications
Who it is for: For everyone
Part 4 of 4 of Start here.

What RIM emails you and why, your Notifications page, and choosing how team news reaches you.

**Step 1. Every email has one way in**

Each RIM email has one button or link, and it takes you to the exact place: the session, the conversation, the file. If you're not signed in on that phone or computer, you'll be asked to sign in first.

**Step 2. Notifications: everything in one place**

Everything RIM emails you is also on your Notifications page, under My RIM. Tap a line to read it.

**Step 3. Open one**

A line opens in place, with its details and one button to go there. Opening it marks it read.

**Step 4. Just one team, or one kind**

From shows news from one team. View shows one kind: Schedule, Conversations, Files, Programs, or Your account.

**Step 5. Choose how team news is emailed**

In My Profile, under Notifications, choose Email me each one, One email a day, or Don't email me team news. Then choose Save email choice.

Tip: **Always sent:** emails about you, your sessions, your registrations and your account come whatever you choose, and so does a session that needs someone within two days.

**The emails you may get**

All of these but the sign-in code, the new-member welcome and the daily summary are also on your Notifications page. The ones marked team news follow the choice you make in My Profile; the rest always come.

*Signing in and joining*

- **Your Rooted In Mindfulness sign-in code**: Your 6-digit code. It works for 30 minutes.
- **Welcome back to Rooted In Mindfulness**: A letter after your first sign-in, if you were a member on the old website. (New members get “Welcome to Rooted In Mindfulness”.)
- **Welcome to [your team]**: You've been added to a team. Open your team takes you to its space.

*Your own sessions (always sent)*

- **You're hosting: [program] · [date]**: You're confirmed for a session, in your team's words (greeting, covering AV, facilitating).
- **Reminder: you're hosting · [program] · [date]**: Two days before your session, with everyone serving it.
- **Your session is covered**: A teammate took the session you asked cover for.
- **You're no longer hosting: [program] · [date]**: A lead took you off a session.
- **Your [team] schedule has changed, or [program] has a new schedule**: Sessions you were on no longer take place, or you're no longer on that team.

*Your regular turns, or rotations (always sent)*

- **You're scheduled for [program]**: A lead put you on a rotation, and these sessions are now yours.
- **You're no longer scheduled for [program] on [date]**: A rotation change took a session from you.
- **You've been removed from the [program] rotation**: A lead took you off a rotation.
- **Your [role] rotation has ended**: A rotation you were on has ended, and its sessions are open.

*When your team needs someone (team news)*

- **Cover needed: [program] on [date]**: A teammate asked for someone to take their session. Cover this session opens it.
- **Needs a host: [program] · [date]**: A session coming up has no one now.
- **[N] sessions in the next two weeks need a host**: Every Monday, the open sessions for the next two weeks.
- **New program added: [program]**: A new program your team may help with.

*Conversations and files (team news)*

- **New conversation in [team]: [title]**: Someone started a topic and asked for you to be told.
- **New reply in [team]: [title]**: A reply in a topic you follow.
- **[Name] shared “[file]” with [team]**: A file shared with your team.
- **[Name] commented on “[file]”**: A comment on a file, for those asked to be told.
- **What's new on your teams**: Only if you chose One email a day: one morning summary in place of the separate emails.

*Programs you register for (always sent)*

- **You're registered — [program]**: Your registration, with the date, place and an Add to calendar link.
- **Your dana receipt for [program]**: A record of your gift, for your taxes.
- **Reminder: [program]**: Before a program you registered for.

*Only for team leads*

- **[Team]: [N] sessions need a decision**: The lead's brief: on Mondays when there's something to decide or something changed, and on other days when a session two days away still needs someone.

**If you need help**

Ask your team's coordinators (they're marked Coordinator on your team's Members page), or write to support@rootedinmindfulness.org and someone will help you.

#### The Scheduler for hosts

Address: rim-next.vercel.app/tutorials/scheduler-for-hosts
Who it is for: For the Hosting Team

Finding your team, taking a session, asking the team to cover, stepping in for a teammate, and what you'll see on the day.

**Step 1. Open your team's Scheduler**

Signed in, go to My Teams and choose Hosting Team. On your team's home, choose Open the Scheduler.

Tip: **The line above the button** tells you how many sessions in the next two weeks still need a host. On a phone, ☰ beside your team's name also lists Scheduler.

On a computer this step reads:
Tip: **The line above the button** tells you how many sessions in the next two weeks still need a host. Scheduler is also in the column on the left, right under Home.

**Step 2. Read the schedule**

Each row is one session: its day and time on the left, then who is hosting. All shows every session, Needs help shows the ones that still need someone, and My schedule shows yours.

Tip: **The small arrow beside My schedule** lets you pick a teammate and see their sessions.

**Step 3. Take a session that needs you**

Host needed means no one has the session yet. Choose Yes, I can host this to take it.

Tip: **“via rotation”** on a session means it comes from a regular turn your coordinators set up.

**Step 4. Say yes**

Check the day and time, then choose Yes, I'll host this. The session is yours, and a confirmation email is on its way.

Tip: **Two days before** each session you're hosting, you'll get a reminder that names everyone serving it.

**Step 5. Can't make it? Ask the team**

Find your session (My schedule shows it) and choose Ask the team to cover. Add a note if you like, then choose Send to the team. Your teammates are told, and the session stays yours until someone steps in.

Tip: **Plans changed again?** Choose Cancel my request on the same row, then Yes, cancel the request. The session stays yours, and the team knows the request is closed.

**Step 6. Step in for a teammate**

When a teammate asks for cover, their session shows their name and needs help. Choose Yes, I can cover, then Yes, I'll cover this. They're told you're stepping in.

**Step 7. When the team needs someone**

Your team's Updates page (on a phone, ☰ then Updates) lists, under Needs you, the sessions in the next two weeks that still need a host. You can take one right there with Take this session.

Tip: **You'll hear by email too.** When a session close at hand loses its person, the team is told, and every Monday brings the next two weeks' open sessions.

**Step 8. On the day**

From 30 minutes before the start, My Home shows Enter Zoom as host on the session you're hosting; from 10 minutes before, it reads Join on Zoom. Either one opens your host page with the code.

Tip: **The Scheduler works too.** In that same window your session shows Enter room.

Link at this step: "Next: the host code and Claim Host in Zoom →" (/tutorials/zoom-for-hosts#step-2)

**If you need help**

Ask your team's coordinators (they're marked Coordinator on your team's Members page), or write to support@rootedinmindfulness.org and someone will help.

#### Leading a session on Zoom, for hosts

Address: rim-next.vercel.app/tutorials/zoom-for-hosts
Who it is for: For the Hosting Team

On the day: opening the room from My Home, the host code, joining Zoom by name (no Zoom account), Claim Host, and stepping in as the backup.

**Step 1. On the day, open My Home**

Sign in to the RIM website and go to My Home. From 30 minutes before the start, the session you're hosting shows Enter Zoom as host; from 10 minutes before, it reads Join on Zoom. Choose it.

Tip: **Settling in early.** The room is yours from 30 minutes before. Everyone else can come in from 10 minutes before.

**Step 2. Note the host code, then join**

This page says You're hosting today and shows the host code. Keep it in view and choose Join as host. Zoom opens. (How to claim host in Zoom, on this page, opens these steps in a new tab.)

Tip: **The code is for the team.** Members never see it, so please don't post it in the chat.

**Step 3. You don't sign in to Zoom**

There's no Zoom account or password to remember. The first time Zoom opens on a phone or computer, it asks for your name. Type your first name and last initial and choose Join. Zoom remembers it next time. (If you're signed in to a Zoom account of your own, Zoom shows that account's name.)

Tip: **The very first time,** your browser may ask “Open Zoom?”: choose Open. A phone may ask you to get the Zoom app first.

**Step 4. In Zoom, open Participants**

Tap Participants in the row of buttons at the bottom of the screen. If the buttons are hidden, tap the screen once and they appear.

On a computer this step reads:
Click Participants in the row of buttons along the bottom of the Zoom window. The list of everyone in the room opens on the right. If the buttons are hidden, move the mouse over the window.

**Step 5. Claim host with the code**

Tap Claim Host, type the code from step 2, and confirm. The host controls are now yours.

Tip: **Don't see Claim Host?** Tap the three dots (•••) at the top of the Participants list and look there. If someone already holds host, that's fine. If no one does and it isn't there, claim host from a computer, or ask your coordinator.

On a computer this step reads:
At the bottom of the Participants list, click Claim host. Type the code from step 2 and click Claim Host. The host controls are now yours.
Tip: **Can't find it?** It may be under More (…) at the bottom of the list. If it isn't there at all, someone already holds host, and that's fine.

**Step 6. If you're the backup**

Helping with a session someone else is hosting? Open your team's Scheduler: from 30 minutes before, the session shows Enter room. This page names who is hosting today and shows the same code. Choose Join. If they're there, there's nothing to do. If they can't come, claim host with the code so the session is never left without one.

[Drawing: The backup's page naming who is leading today, with Join marked.]

Tip: **Only one host at a time.** Whoever holds host can make another person a co-host inside Zoom.

**If you need help**

If Zoom doesn't open, choose Join as host again. If the website says the session can't open right now, wait a moment and choose Try again.

Anything else: ask your team's coordinators, or write to support@rootedinmindfulness.org and we'll help you in.

#### The Scheduler for AV

Address: rim-next.vercel.app/tutorials/scheduler-for-av
Who it is for: For the Audio/Visual team

Finding your team, taking a session, asking the team to cover, stepping in for a teammate, and what you'll see on the day.

**Step 1. Open your team's Scheduler**

Signed in, go to My Teams and choose Audio/Visual. On your team's home, choose Open the Scheduler.

Tip: **The line above the button** tells you how many sessions in the next two weeks still need AV. On a phone, ☰ beside your team's name also lists Scheduler.

On a computer this step reads:
Tip: **The line above the button** tells you how many sessions in the next two weeks still need AV. Scheduler is also in the column on the left, right under Home.

**Step 2. Read the schedule**

Each row is one session: its day and time on the left, then who is covering AV. All shows every session, Needs help shows the ones that still need someone, and My schedule shows yours.

Tip: **The small arrow beside My schedule** lets you pick a teammate and see their sessions.

**Step 3. Take a session that needs you**

AV needed means no one has the session yet. Choose Yes, I can cover AV to take it.

Tip: **“via rotation”** on a session means it comes from a regular turn your coordinators set up.

**Step 4. Say yes**

Check the day and time, then choose Yes, I'll cover AV. The session is yours, and a confirmation email is on its way.

Tip: **Two days before** each session you're covering AV, you'll get a reminder that names everyone serving it.

**Step 5. Can't make it? Ask the team**

Find your session (My schedule shows it) and choose Ask the team to cover. Add a note if you like, then choose Send to the team. Your teammates are told, and the session stays yours until someone steps in.

Tip: **Plans changed again?** Choose Cancel my request on the same row, then Yes, cancel the request. The session stays yours, and the team knows the request is closed.

**Step 6. Step in for a teammate**

When a teammate asks for cover, their session shows their name and needs help. Choose Yes, I can cover, then Yes, I'll cover this. They're told you're stepping in.

**Step 7. When the team needs someone**

Your team's Updates page (on a phone, ☰ then Updates) lists, under Needs you, the sessions in the next two weeks that still need AV. You can take one right there with Take this session.

Tip: **You'll hear by email too.** When a session close at hand loses its person, the team is told, and every Monday brings the next two weeks' open sessions.

**Step 8. On the day**

On the day, My Home's Today shows the session with “You're covering AV”. Your part happens in person at RIM, so there's nothing you need to open.

Tip: **Two days before,** you'll get a reminder that names everyone serving that session.

**If you need help**

Ask your team's coordinators (they're marked Coordinator on your team's Members page), or write to support@rootedinmindfulness.org and someone will help.

#### Looking after the people

Address: rim-next.vercel.app/tutorials/looking-after-the-people
Who it is for: For the Registration Team

The Registration app: what needs you, a program's people, adding someone, the waiting list, dana, cancelling, messages, settings and History.

**Step 1. Open Registration**

On the Registration Team's Home, choose Open Registration.

Tip: **Don't see Registration?** Registration opens only for registrars, the guiding teacher and admins, because it holds people's details.

**Step 2. What needs you**

Registration opens on Needs you: anything across every program that is waiting for a person, such as "A place is open, and 2 people are waiting." Each has a button that takes you to them. Below are Going out soon, the reminders and notes that send themselves, and Programs with registration on.

[Drawing: Needs you, with See who's waiting marked.]

**Step 3. A program's people**

Choose a program. The top line says where it stands, such as "Open until Nov 6 · 12 of 20 places taken." Under People, the buttons show everyone, or only those Registered, Waiting, with Dana not finished, or Cancelled. Search finds a name or email.

**Step 4. A person's card**

Choose a name to open their card: Contact, Their place, Dana, Their answers, Notes for the registration team, and History. Each part has its own buttons.

**Step 5. It asks first**

Anything that emails someone, or changes their place or their dana, asks first in plain words, and says whether they will be emailed. Choose the button that begins with Yes to go ahead, or Never mind. The answer appears right there.

**Step 6. Add someone who phoned**

Choose Add a person. Fill in their name, email and phone, and their answers. If the program invites dana, choose They'll give online, Given by cash or check, or No dana. Choose Add them.

Tip: **Full?** They join the waiting list instead. If they'll give online, they get the link to give first, and the confirmation follows.

**Step 7. Give a place to someone waiting**

Choose Waiting, open the card of the person first in line, and choose Offer them a place, then Yes, register and email them. They're told their place is confirmed.

**Step 8. Dana given another way**

Choose Dana not finished and open their card. Under Dana, choose Given another way, then Yes, mark it received. Or choose Send a dana reminder, or No dana for them.

**Step 9. Cancel, or put back**

On their card, choose Cancel their registration. The box to email them is ticked; untick it if you've told them yourself. Choose Yes, cancel it.

Tip: **A mistake?** Choose Cancelled, open their card, and choose Restore their registration.

**Step 10. Message people**

Choose Message people. Choose who it goes to, write a subject and a message, and choose Send me a test. Read it in your inbox, then send it. Replies come to the registrar's inbox.

Tip: **More than 50 people?** Use the program's Flodesk mailing list instead.

**Step 11. Registration settings**

Choose Registration settings to open it. Set Registration (Open, Not open yet or Closed), Opens, Places, Closes and the Mailing list, then choose Save settings. Times are Central. Closing never takes anyone's place away.

**Step 12. History, the sign-in list and the spreadsheet**

History lists everything that happened, and who did it. Print a sign-in list gives names and a box to tick, for the door. Download a spreadsheet gives everyone and their answers.

### Leading your team

Address: rim-next.vercel.app/tutorials/leading-your-team
Who it is for: For team leads

Your team's line, Members (who hasn't signed in, adding, pausing), Leads only files, removal requests, and your Monday brief.

**Step 1. Your team's line**

On your team's home, choose Write this team's line (or Edit this line). Type one sentence about the team, for everyone on it, and choose Save.

**Step 2. See who hasn't signed in**

On Members, anyone who hasn't signed in to the new site yet shows “Hasn't signed in yet.” Until they do, they get none of the team's emails. A friendly word from you is often all it takes.

Tip: **Only leads see this.** Members don't see each other's sign-in or email addresses.

**Step 3. Add someone to the team**

Choose + Add Member, type at least three letters of their name or email, and choose them in the list.

Tip: **Not in the list?** Someone who hasn't signed in to the new site yet won't appear. An admin or the registrar can add them from the Member Registry.

**Step 4. Change a member's settings**

Choose Edit beside a name. You can set their Status, make them a Coordinator, choose Can take sessions on the schedule and Receives team notifications, and keep a private Coordinator note. Choose Save.

**Step 5. Paused or Inactive**

Paused is for a break. If they have sessions coming, you're asked: Keep their sessions, or Take them off their sessions so the team can take them. Inactive means they've left the team: their sessions open, their rotations end, and the team hears about any in the next two weeks.

Tip: **Back again?** Set them to Active. Their pause note clears.

**Step 6. Files for leads only**

In Files, you'll see Team files and Leads only. Leads only is for the team's leads. To share a file with everyone, open its ⋯ menu and choose Move to Team files (or Move to Leads only to keep one among the leads).

**Step 7. Approve or keep a removal**

When someone asks to remove a file, it waits under Pending removal. Approve removal sends it to Google's trash; if that was a mistake, write to support within 30 days to get it back. Keep leaves it in place.

**Step 8. Your Monday brief**

On Mondays when there's something to decide or something changed, you get one email laid out for decisions: each open session, why it's open, who on the team can take it, and what changed since last Monday. On other days it comes only when a session two days away still needs someone.

Tip: **Every new topic too:** as a lead, you're told about every new conversation on your team.

### The Scheduler for leads

Address: rim-next.vercel.app/tutorials/scheduler-for-leads
Who it is for: For leads of the Scheduler teams

Assigning and removing, cover requests, greeters' sign-ups, and setting up, changing and ending rotations.

**Step 1. What a lead sees**

As a lead, the Scheduler has two tabs: Schedule and Rotations. On Schedule, Needs help turns amber when sessions still need someone; choose it to see just those.

Tip: **Greeter leads:** the amber Needs help is for teams with one person per session. On an open sign-up team, people sign up themselves.

**Step 2. Assign someone to an open session**

On a session marked Host needed (in your team's words), choose Assign someone…, then choose a person from the list. They're emailed a confirmation.

[Drawing: An open session with Assign someone… marked.]

**Step 3. Manage coverage**

On a session someone has, choose Manage coverage. Ask the team to cover asks for a replacement while they stay on it. Remove takes them off, and Reassign to me puts you on it instead.

**Step 4. Remove someone from a session**

Remove asks first. The session opens again, the person is told, and if it's within two weeks the team hears that it needs someone. Choose Yes, remove, or Keep them on.

**Step 5. Clear a cover request**

When someone asked for cover but will make it after all, choose Clear request on their session, then Yes, clear the request. The request closes and they keep the session.

**Step 6. Greeter leads: remove a sign-up**

On an open sign-up team, there's no assigning or cover requests. Choose Remove beside a person's name, then Yes, remove. They're told, and if no one is left and the session is within two weeks, the team hears.

**Step 7. Rotations: the regular turns**

Rotations fill sessions automatically from a pattern. Each program shows a row for each day it meets, with who has each week of the month. Choose Set up on a day with no rotation yet, or Edit on one already set.

**Step 8. Set up a rotation**

Choose a Pattern: Same every week, Every other week, Alternate by week of the month, or Custom. Choose the person (or people), check the Preview, and choose Save & apply.

Tip: **5th weeks** are optional: add a 5th-week person, or leave it and that week is covered or skipped as the form says.

**Step 9. Every other week**

For two people taking turns week by week, choose Every other week. Under Starting, choose the first person's first session; then choose the First person and the Second person.

**Step 10. Saved, and what it filled**

After Save & apply, a note says the day's rotation is saved, how many sessions it filled, and who has the next ones.

Tip: **Someone already on a date?** You're asked first: Leave them alone, Replace all, or Decide one by one. Cover requests and people who took a session themselves are kept unless you choose otherwise.

**Step 11. Change or end a rotation**

Choose Reset Tuesday (the day's name) to change it: Remove from rotation takes one person off, Set end date keeps sessions up to a date, and Reset that day's rotation clears it. Everyone affected is emailed.

Tip: **Just one date?** If someone can't make one session, they use Ask the team to cover on the Schedule tab instead (greeters cancel that sign-up).

**If you need help**

Questions about leading your team: write to support@rootedinmindfulness.org, or ask Jesse.
