# Programs and Registration

This file explains how RIM sets up its programs and looks after the people who register for them. It is written for the RIM Guide to answer volunteers, especially the registration team. It describes the site as it is now.

The site is at rim-next.vercel.app until the domain moves. The page addresses below start after the domain, for example `/tools/registration`.

---

## Two apps, two jobs

RIM keeps setting up a program and looking after its people in two separate apps.

- **Programs** (`/tools/programs`, also called the Program Manager) is where a program is set up: its words, dates, place, teachers, teams, how people join, the emails it sends, dana, and where it appears on the site.
- **Registration** (`/tools/registration`) is where the registration team looks after the people: who is coming, who is waiting, dana, their answers, notes, messages and history.

**Why two:** setting up a program and caring for its people are different kinds of work, often done by different people. Keeping people's details in one app, open only to a few roles, protects their privacy while more people help with setup.

### Who can open each

- **Programs** opens for the Registrar, Admin and Guiding Teacher roles, and for active members of the two teams that set programs up: the Registration Team and Program Central. Programs never shows anyone's details. Someone without a registration role sees each program's capacity ("20 places" or "No limit"), not how many are taken or by whom.
- Some Programs actions stay with the Registrar, Admin and Guiding Teacher roles only: archiving, restoring and deleting a program, making a new guest link, the old categories page, deciding who asks each question, and choosing a team that prepares.
- **Registration** opens only for the Registrar, Admin and Guiding Teacher roles. Being on the Registration Team is not enough by itself, because the app holds people's contact details, answers and dana. If someone says "I don't see Registration," this is almost always why.
- **Dana amounts** (how much someone gave) are seen only by registrars, the guiding teacher and admins. Helpers, teachers and teams never see dana.

### Getting there

- From the Registration Team's space: Home shows Registration as the team's tool in one card, with the line "N new registrations in the last seven days" and the button **Open Registration**. The Program Manager is listed below as a supporting app.
- On a phone, the team's menu (☰ beside the team's name) lists the Program Manager and Registration.
- In the Programs list, each row has **Edit** (opens the editor). The Registrar, Admin and Guiding Teacher roles also see **People** (opens that program in Registration) and **Archive**.

## The Programs list

Each program is one calm row: its name, anything that needs a look, its kind, its registration state, and its places.

**Registration states on the list:**
- **Off**: the program doesn't take sign-ups. People simply come (Open entry), or it isn't ready yet.
- **Open**: taking sign-ups now.
- **Closed**: closed by hand, its closing date has passed, or it is a one-time program that has already ended.
- **Opens Nov 1, 2026** (for example): registration is set to open on that date.

**Places** read in words. The registration roles see "12 of 20 places" (or "12 registered" when there is no limit). Others see only the capacity, "20 places" or "No limit".

Filter pills across the top narrow the list, each with a count. Archived programs have their own view, most recently archived first. If Delete is refused, the page says why (for example, people have registered).

## Starting a program

Choosing **+ Add Program** opens a page that asks **What are you offering?** Since October 28, 2026, choosing a shape, a blank program or a copy starts a **draft** and walks you through setting it up, one question at a time (below, "Setting up a program, step by step"). Drafts already started are listed first, under **Being set up**, and at the top of the Programs list, each with **Continue setting it up**.

### The six shapes

Each shape fills in sensible starting settings. Nothing is saved until Save, and everything can be changed.

- **Drop-in**: Open entry, online: people come as they are, with no registration and no questions.
- **Class**: Open entry, online, meeting weekly, with no registration.
- **Course**: registration for the whole series, meeting weekly, the teacher's two questions, dana invited, a reminder 2 days before.
- **Workshop**: registration, the teacher's two questions, dana invited, a reminder 2 days before.
- **Day of mindfulness**: registration, in person, questions about arriving and dietary needs, dana invited, a reminder 2 days before.
- **Retreat**: registration, in person, the teacher's two questions and the questions about arriving and dietary needs, dana invited, a reminder 2 days before.

The teacher's two questions are "Have you practiced meditation before?" and "Is there anything I should know before we sit together?"

There is also **Start with a blank program →**.

The shape is only a starting point. After the first save, nothing is decided by the shape. No shape fills in a capacity. No shape ticks the greeters or other scheduled teams, because ticking a team emails that team when you save, and a first draft shouldn't do that.

### Or copy a program

Under **Or copy a program**, choose a past program and Copy. This is the easiest way to set up next year's retreat.

- **Kept:** the words, the questions (as new questions, with the old answers staying with the original), the teachers, the teams, dana and the settings.
- **Never copied:** the people who registered, the dates, the notice, the guest link, the closing and opening dates, and the mailing-list segment. The copy also gets a new web address (the old one with a number added), unless you change it.
- Save asks for the dates if they are missing ("Under When and where: set this program's dates. A copy starts without them.").
- A note at the top says what was and wasn't copied.

When a new program is published, the editor opens on it and says **"Created. It's saved."**

## Setting up a program, step by step

A new program is set up one question at a time, in the seven sections below, in the order you'd decide them. Wording is provisional until Jesse's read-aloud.

- **One question, one Continue.** Each question says where you are ("When and where · 3 of 8"). **Back** goes to the question before; **Skip this for now** appears on optional questions. A question that needs something before going on says what, beside the button.
- **Your answers so far** sit beside the question on a computer, or folded above it on a phone. Tap any answer to go back to it. Changing your mind keeps whatever still fits; if something no longer fits, it says so ("The first session was Friday, October 30, which doesn't fit now, so you'll choose it again.") and marks that answer.
- **It's a draft until you publish.** It's saved as you go ("Saved as you go."), so you can stop and come back. No one sees it and no team is told anything until Publish.
- **When and where**, so the schedule can't go wrong: **How often** (Once, Several days in a row, Every week, Every other week, Once a month); **which days** (for monthly, which one of the month and which day); **what time**, with the length shown ("That's 45 minutes."); **the first session**, on a calendar where only the days it meets can be chosen, from today on; **when it ends** (It goes on, After a number of sessions, On a date), with the sessions listed as you set them ("Fridays, 7 to 7:45 PM. 4 sessions." and the dates).
- **Where should people find it?** shows the section of Programs & Events as visitors will see it, with this program in its place, and **Show it first in its section**. My Home can wait for a date ("Only from a date…").
- **The review** shows every answer as a sentence, each section with **Change**, and **When you publish**: what publishing sets off (where it appears, registration opening, which teams are told, a Zoom meeting for each session). **Publish** stays off while something is missing, and each missing thing links to its question. **Keep it as a draft** leaves it for later; **Discard this draft** asks first.
- After Publish, the program's page opens on it ("Published. It's saved"), where each section has its own Change.

## A program's page, and changing it

Since October 28, 2026, a published program has its own page in Programs (choose its name, or **Edit**, in the Programs list). It replaced the one long editor. Wording is provisional until Jesse's read-aloud.

- **At the top:** its name, whether it's published or archived, where it's listed ("on Programs & Events and This Week"), and for the registration roles how many people are registered. Below that, **View its page** (opens the public page), **People** (Registration), and **Post a notice**.
- **Seven cards, one per section**, each saying what's set in plain sentences ("Fridays, 7 to 7:45 PM. 4 sessions." with the dates; "People register first."; "12 places, then a waiting list."). Each card has **Change**.
- **Worth a look:** anything a program needs and doesn't have yet (no description, no category) is listed above the cards, each one opening its section.
- **Change** walks just that section's questions, the same ones as when a program is set up (below, "The questions in each section"). Nothing is saved while you go. The last question's button reads **See what this changes**.
- **Before you save** says what the change will do, before anything happens: sessions that no longer happen and new ones (with dates), the people on sessions that are cleared (they're told), rotations that end (the team's leads set up the new days), the teams that are told, a new time for every session, a new name reaching every copy, and the people registered who haven't heard (with an update offered after saving). When nothing else is affected it says "This changes only what you see above. No one is emailed." Choose **Save this change**, **Back to the questions**, or **Cancel the change**.
- **Safe saves:** only what you changed is sent. If someone else changed the same thing while you had the page open (another person, or Registration's settings), nothing is saved and the message names it. Changes to other things are kept.
- After a save, a note at the top says what happened ("Saved at 3:41 PM."), with any sessions cleared, Zoom meetings not yet renamed, or online sessions that overlap on Zoom.
- **The guest link** (when guests can join) shows in How people join, with **Copy the link**, and for the registrar, admins and the guiding teacher **Reset the link** (it asks first; the old link stops working).
- **The web address** stays as it was when a program is renamed: it links the program to its people and hosts.

### The order on Programs & Events

There's no number to set. Each section lists programs that go on by their first day of the week (Monday first) and time, then programs with an end by their first session. A program can be **shown first** in its section: in its Where it appears ("Show it first in its section"), or on **How each section of Programs & Events is ordered** (a link at the top of the Programs list), which shows every section as visitors see it, with **Show first**, **Stop showing first**, and **Move up** and **Move down** among those shown first. The Kalyana Mitta groups page lists Community Groups the same way.

### The questions in each section

The same questions set a program up and change it.

1. **About:** What is it called? (it appears on the site, My Home and every email; its address is made from the name). Say what it is in one line. Tell people about it (the description, written for someone who has never been to RIM, and a picture). Anything more for its page? (a short quote and who said it, notes such as accessibility).
2. **When and where:** Where does it meet? (In person, Online, Both). Where, in person? (At RIM, or somewhere else with its address and a map link). Which online room? (RIM's Zoom, with **Record the sessions**; or someone else's meeting, with its link). How often does it meet? Which days (or which one of the month)? What time (with its length)? The first session (only days it meets can be chosen). When does it end? A line for the day itself (shown on My Home on the day).
   - **What a save won't take**, so a schedule can't mean something other than it says: a weekly program with no day; a first session on a day it doesn't meet; an end on a later day than the start for a repeating program; every 3 weeks or more, or Daily, for a new schedule; a series of fewer than 2 sessions; an end before it begins. The number of sessions counts real sessions: 4 on Mondays and Wednesdays is Monday, Wednesday, Monday, Wednesday. A "4th" of the month starts in a month with five of that day (otherwise it's read as the last).
3. **Teachers and teams:** Who teaches it? (or "No teacher for this one"). Does a host run the online room? (a hosting team, or No host needed: it's self-led). Which teams help at each session? (greeters, AV). Does a team prepare for it? (a kitchen team; "They can reach people" shows them email and phone; only the registration roles choose these, because that decides who sees people's details; dana is never shown). **Teams hear when it's published or saved.**
4. **How people join:** They just come (open entry: anyone signed in may join, marked Drop-in on This Week), They register first, or Both. **Open entry is the only setting that decides access**; Category and Format are labels. And people without a RIM account? (only members, a guest link that works around session times, or for someone else's meeting a Join button on the program's page when no one registers). When can people register? (as soon as it's published or from a date; until it begins or until a date). How many places? (no limit, or a number, then a waiting list; never fewer than the places taken). What would you like to ask them? (each question's words, its kind of answer (a short answer, a longer answer, yes or no, a choice), whether it needs an answer, and **Asked by**: the registration team, the teacher, or a team that prepares. The registration team reads every answer; only the registration roles change Asked by. Old answers are never lost).
5. **Messages to those who register** (only when people register): What should the confirmation say? (what to bring, directions, a link; a Google Doc needs "Anyone with the link"). When should the reminder go? (days before the first session, in the morning and to anyone who registers later; on a set date; or no reminder; with anything to add). A note the day after it ends? (for one-time programs only).
6. **Dana:** How is dana offered? (No dana step; Dana invited, with a suggested amount; A set amount, plus dana; A fixed amount, not RIM's usual practice). What do people read when they give? (starting words to copy in, and a short note near the Register button). **Dana at RIM is voluntary**; program dana is shared equally between RIM and the Teaching Fund, which the registration step and program page already say.
7. **Where it appears:** Which of RIM's ways does it belong to? (Foundations, Ongoing Learning & Practice, Immersion, Community Group, Special Event; Service and Private are never listed). What kind of offering is it? (Drop-in, Class, Course, Workshop, Day of mindfulness, Retreat; a silent sit; hosted by volunteers). Where should people find it? (Programs & Events, with the section shown and **Show it first**; This Week; My Home on the days it meets, optionally only from a date; for a one-time program, taken down once it's over).

Private programs are left off This Week.

### Tell the people registered?

People who registered have the time, place and confirmation as they were when they signed up. Changing the program doesn't reach them by itself.

So when a saved change moves the schedule, the format, the place, or the confirmation message, and people have a place, a panel appears at the top of the program's page: **Tell the people registered?** It names what changed and drafts an email with the new details. The subject is "A change to [program]". You can rewrite both.

- **Send me a test**: read it in your own inbox first.
- **Send to the N people registered**: asks first, then sends. Replies go to the registrar's inbox, and it's kept in History.
- **Not now**: closes it.

Only registrars, the guiding teacher and admins see the count and the draft. Anyone else is told the registration team needs to send it. For more than 50 people, the panel says to use the program's Flodesk mailing list instead.

### Post a notice

For news people need right away, such as "Cancelled tonight," choose **Post a notice** on the program's page. Type one line, choose **Show until** (the last day it shows), and choose **Post the notice**.

The notice shows on the program's page, on Programs & Events and This Week, and on My Home. It clears itself after the Show until day. Registrars can also tick **Also email the N people registered**. The notice saves on its own. An empty notice takes it down.

## What a registrant experiences

1. **The program page** shows a Register button when registration is open, the opening date when it isn't open yet, or that it's closed.
2. **The form** (`/programs/[program]/register`): name, email, phone (optional), the program's questions, and agreement to the Community Care Agreements for someone new. A signed-in member's details are filled in. Someone without an account can register as a guest; registering makes them a member, and they agree to the terms at their first sign-in.
3. **The dana step**, if the program invites dana. With Voluntary dana, the registration is already real; giving is an invitation beside it. They can give through Stripe, or choose not to give at this time.
4. **Payment** goes through Stripe's checkout page. After paying, they land on a thank-you page that says they're registered, thanks them with the real amount, and says two emails are on the way.
5. **Emails:** the confirmation (with the confirmation message, calendar links and, for another venue, "Map and directions") and, when they gave, a dana receipt with RIM's legal name for their records.
6. **If they walk away from the dana step**, the registration stands. After a day, it's marked as no dana and the confirmation goes out.
7. **When the program is full**, they join the waiting list and get an email saying so.
8. **When a place opens for them**, the registration team offers it, and they get an email saying their place is confirmed. A guest can finish their dana from the email's link without signing in.

**Members can't cancel their own registration.** This is a standing decision. They contact the registration team (support@rootedinmindfulness.org), and the team cancels it in Registration.

Every real registration also sends a notice to support@ with a button that opens it in Registration.

## The Registration app

### The desk

Registration opens on the desk: "What needs a person, then every program with registration on."

- **Needs you**: anything across every program that's waiting for a person, each with a button that goes to them. For example: "2 places are open, and 2 people are waiting." (See who's waiting), "3 people haven't finished their dana." (See who), or "N haven't had the reminder."
- **Going out soon**: the automatic emails due in the next two weeks, with the real day and time. These are the reminders and the note after, so nothing automatic goes out unseen.
- **Programs with registration on**, and a fold of other programs that have registrations.

### A program's page

Choosing a program opens its page.

- **The top line** says where it stands, such as "Open until Nov 6 · 12 of 20 places taken · 2 waiting." Other states: "Registration off," "Closed," "Closed on [date]," "Opens [date and time]."
- **Needs you** for this program, or "Nothing needs you for this program."
- It says when the reminder goes, or went and to how many ("went out to N people; the M people who haven't had it yet get it [day]"), and when the note after goes.
- **Send the reminder now to N people**: shows while a morning is still left before the program. It asks first and names the people. They won't get it again automatically.
- **Two tabs: People and History.**

### People

Filter pills: **All**, **Registered**, **Waiting**, **Dana not finished**, **Cancelled**. **Search** finds a name or email. Each row shows the name, their place, their dana state, and the date they registered.

Choosing a name opens **their card**:
- **Contact**: email and phone.
- **Their place**: registered, waiting (with their number in line), or cancelled.
- **Dana**: for example "Received," "Not finished," "Given another way," or no dana. Amounts show only to registrars, the guiding teacher and admins: "Amounts given are seen only by registrars, the guiding teacher and admins."
- **Their answers**: answers to the program's questions, which the team can edit (**Save their answers**), or **Send them a link to change their answers** by email.
- **Notes**: **Add a note for the registration team**. Each note keeps its author and date. Notes are never edited after they're written. An older note from the previous system shows as "An earlier note."
- **History** for that person.

**It asks first.** Anything that emails someone, or changes their place or their dana, asks first in plain words and says whether they'll be emailed. The button that goes ahead begins with Yes ("Yes, register and email them," "Yes, mark it received," "Yes, cancel it"); **Never mind** backs out. The answer appears right there.

**Common actions on a card:**
- **Offer them a place** (someone waiting): then "Yes, register and email them." They're told their place is confirmed.
- **Given another way** (dana by cash or check): then "Yes, mark it received." If they hadn't had their confirmation yet (their dana was still open), it goes to them now.
- **Send a dana reminder**: emails the link to give.
- **No dana for them**: then "Yes, no dana." If they hadn't had their confirmation yet, it goes to them now. The box says so before you choose.
- **Send the confirmation again**: for example after the confirmation message changed. It isn't offered while someone's dana is still open, because their first confirmation hasn't gone yet; it goes when the dana is settled.
- If someone else settled the dana while the page was open, the card says "Their dana was already settled. Reload the page to see where it stands." and nothing is changed.
- **Send the reminder** (or again).
- **Cancel their registration**: the box to email them is ticked; untick it if you've already told them. Then "Yes, cancel it."
- **Restore their registration**: from Cancelled, to undo a mistake.
- **Delete the record**: only for a cancelled registration. Its history stays, with a line saying it was deleted.

### Add a person

**Add a person** is "For someone who phoned, wrote, or signed up on paper. If their email belongs to a member, it's added to their account."

- First name, last name, email, phone (optional), and their answers to the program's questions.
- **Dana** (if the program invites it), starting with nothing chosen:
  - **They'll give online**: they get an email with the link to give, and their confirmation follows once they give or decline.
  - **Given by cash or check**: marked received.
  - **No dana**.
- **Email them their confirmation email**: ticked by default.
- **Add them**.

If every place is taken, they join the waiting list instead, and you can offer them a place from their card. If they're already registered, waiting or cancelled, it says where they are instead of adding them twice. Staff can add a person after registration has closed, or before it opens. A program with registration off, or archived, can't take additions. Nobody is charged by Add a person.

### Message people

**Message people** sends "An email from the registration team about [program]. Replies come to the registrar's inbox, and it's kept in History."

- **To**: everyone registered, everyone waiting, or both.
- Subject and message. It begins "Hi" and their first name, and ends with a link to the program.
- **Send me a test** first, read it, then **Send to N people**.
- Up to 50 people. For more, use the program's Flodesk mailing list.

### Registration settings

Folded under Needs you, **Registration settings** shows a summary such as "Open · 14 places · closes Nov 6, 2026 · mailing list on." Opened, it has:

- **Registration**: Open, Not open yet, or Closed. (Turning registration off altogether is done on the program's page: How people join, "They just come".)
- **Opens**: for Not open yet, a date and time still ahead.
- **Places**: leave empty for no limit. It won't go below the places already taken.
- **Closes**: the closing date. Leave empty to stay open until you close it.
- **Mailing list**: Automatic, On, or Off, and the **Flodesk segment**. "Make a new segment at the first registration" creates one named "Name MM/DD/YY". "Use an existing segment instead" links one Flodesk already has. With the mailing list off, no one is added to Flodesk.
- **Save settings**.

Times are Central. Closing never takes anyone's place away ("Everyone registered keeps their place."). The mailing list lives only here, not on the program's page.

### History, the sign-in list and the spreadsheet

- **History** lists everything that happened to the program's registrations, and who did it. Things a person did themselves (registering, giving dana, changing their answers) read under their own name. Things the site did read as "RIM …".
- **Print a sign-in list**: names in order of last name with a box to tick, for the door. No contact details, answers or dana. Printing it is kept in History.
- **Download a spreadsheet**: everyone with their answers, a column per question. Downloading is kept in History.

## How helpers see registrations

Three pages let the people helping with a program see what their part needs, and nothing more. None of them ever shows dana.

- **Teaching** (`/account/teaching`, "Teaching" in the personal menu, for teachers of a current program and the guiding teacher). Each program's page shows how many are coming, **First registration here** for someone who has never registered on this site, **What they told you** (answers to the teacher's own questions, and registration-team notes marked for the teacher), and **Everyone coming** with each person's email and phone. People waiting appear by name only. On My Home, a program the person teaches links "Who's coming →".
- **Today's list** (`/account/greeting/...`, linked from a greeter's or other supporting team's line on My Home on their day). It lists the people with a place for that session, their answers to that team's own questions, and an **Arrived** button for each. It has no contact details. Under the list: "Someone not on the list? Welcome them, and tell the registration team their name." For a program people don't register for, it says to welcome everyone who comes.
- **Helping with** (`/tools/helping`, the app for teams that prepare, such as a kitchen team). It shows the programs the team is ticked on, how many are coming, and the answers to the team's own questions. Email and phone show only if "Can reach people" is ticked.

**The 30-day window:** a teacher's page and Helping with close their list of people **30 days after a program ends**, which leaves time for a thank-you and any follow-up. Meanwhile the program is listed under **"Ended."** A one-time program ends on its last day. A series with a set number of sessions ends on its last session. An ongoing program ends when it's archived. The registration team and the guiding teacher keep access as always.

## When registration closes by itself

Registration is taking sign-ups only when all of these hold:
1. It isn't Off.
2. It isn't Closed by hand.
3. Its closing date (Registration Deadline, or Closes in settings) hasn't passed.
4. For a one-time program, the program hasn't ended.
5. Its opening date, if set, has arrived.

So a workshop stops offering Register once it's over, even before it's archived the next morning. A repeating program isn't closed by the end rule.

**The opening and closing dates never change who can get into a session.** That is decided only by registration (having a place) and Open entry.

## Common questions: "why does it say…?"

- **"Off" on the Programs list.** The program doesn't take sign-ups. If people should simply come, check that Open entry is on.
- **"Closed" but I never closed it.** Its closing date passed, or it's a one-time program that has ended. To reopen, move or clear the date in Registration settings.
- **"Opens Nov 1, 2026."** It's set to Not open yet with that opening date. The program page says when it opens, with no form until then.
- **"12 of 20 places taken."** Twelve people have a place out of a capacity of 20. People waiting are counted separately ("2 waiting"). A place held while someone is paying online counts toward the 20, but isn't a registration until they finish.
- **"Drop-in" on This Week.** The program has Open entry, so anyone signed in can come without registering. A program can show Drop-in and still offer Register, when registration and Open entry are both on.
- **"Dana not finished."** The person is registered but hasn't given or declined yet. After a day it's marked no dana and their confirmation goes out. The team can also mark it given another way, send a reminder, or choose no dana.
- **"N haven't had the reminder."** They registered after the reminder went out, or it hasn't gone yet. They'll get it the next morning automatically, or choose Send the reminder now.
- **The program isn't on Programs & Events.** Open the program's page: the line at the top says whether it's on Programs & Events, its Where it appears card says why not (hidden, Private or Service, no Category), and **Worth a look** lists anything missing. A one-time program whose date has passed leaves the listings by itself.
- **A save was refused because "someone else changed" a field.** Another person, or Registration's settings, changed that same field while the page was open. Reload to see their change, then make yours again.
- **The people registered didn't hear about a time change.** Changing the program doesn't email them. Use Tell the people registered? after saving, or Message people in Registration.
- **The Programs list shows places but not names.** Only the registration roles see how many registered and who. People's details live in Registration.
- **A teacher can't see the list any more.** The program ended more than 30 days ago, and its list of who came has closed to the teacher.
- **The web address (slug) can't be changed.** People have registered, or the schedule uses it.
- **A member asks how to cancel.** Members can't cancel their own registration; the registration team cancels it for them.
