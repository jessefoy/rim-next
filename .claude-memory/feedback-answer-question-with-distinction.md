When Jesse's answer to a question is itself a question ("You don't think this is useful information?", "You mean if someone cancels and requests a sub?"), stop. Answer it honestly: give the concrete distinction between the cases in plain terms, change the recommendation if his question shows it was wrong, and ask again. Never proceed on a guess.

**Why:** 2026-10-20, on who sees that someone left a session: his first question back made me split leads from the team (useful info goes to who acts on it). His second showed the options blurred "asking for a sub" with "dropping outright". Spelling out the two cases got a clear ruling ("Leads and the person").

**How to apply:** one short paragraph per case, with a concrete example from RIM's real data (a named session, a named role), then the same question re-asked with the recommendation first. Related: [[feedback-ask-flags-as-questions]], [[user-dictation]].
