Jesse often dictates his messages, so words can arrive as speech-to-text slips: on 2026-10-12 "the number area" meant **the member area**, and sentences sometimes end mid-thought ("…when it's ready, and"). Jesse confirmed this for memory on 2026-10-12.

**Why:** reading a slip literally sends the work the wrong way; stopping to ask about every odd word slows him down.

**How to apply:** read by context and the work in hand. When a slip could change what gets built, say the reading in one line ("I'll read 'number area' as the member area") and carry on. When a sentence breaks off, treat what is there as complete unless the missing part changes the work, and say so. Related: [[user-jesse]], [[feedback-ask-flags-as-questions]].
