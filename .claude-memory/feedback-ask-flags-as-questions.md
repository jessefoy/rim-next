When a copy or design pass leaves rulings only Jesse can make (a read-aloud's flags, his earlier rulings meeting his new words), ask them **in chat as questions**, a few at a time, each with the options and my recommendation first. He asked for this on 2026-10-08 after I delivered the home-page read-aloud as a document with twelve flags at the top: "For greater ease, can you ask me each question here, and I'll reference the document? And answer it." Fourteen rulings landed in four rounds.

**Why:** the document is the right carrier for the copy (one markdown file in visitor order, per the house rules), but a flags list makes him do the sorting; a question with a recommendation lets him rule in one tap and add nuance in his own words ("I don't like the word glimpse").

**How to apply:** deliver the copy as the file, then open the rulings as AskUserQuestion batches in page order, recommendation first. Where he answers "I don't know," give the call with the reason and ask once more. Record every ruling verbatim and dated in the vault (the working draft's log, the master reference's Appendix A).
