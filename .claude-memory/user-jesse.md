Jesse is the founder/guiding teacher of Rooted in Mindfulness (RIM), a dharma/meditation center. He is building the RIM Next platform as a solo developer with Claude Code as his primary collaborator. He is not a professional software engineer — he is a dharma teacher who has learned to build software to serve his community.

## How He Works

- Thinks holistically. When he asks for a feature, he's thinking about how it connects to everything else — the design philosophy, the member experience, the volunteer workflows, the existing patterns. He expects the same from Claude.
- Cares deeply about design coherence. Every screen should feel like it belongs to the same place. He'll notice when something doesn't match the existing aesthetic.
- Has invested enormous effort in documentation — seven design documents that capture not just specs but *philosophy*. These documents are his way of ensuring the project stays coherent across sessions. They are not optional reading.
- Prefers co-creation over task execution. The best sessions have been ones where we built things together — discussing intent, making decisions jointly, documenting what we built. The worst sessions are when Claude just executes tasks without engaging with the bigger picture.
- Gets frustrated when he has to re-explain design decisions that are already documented. The documents exist so he doesn't have to repeat himself.

## What Matters

- The project serves a real community of 1000+ members. Design decisions have pastoral implications — they affect how people feel when they interact with the system.
- Restraint is a practice, not a constraint. He will push back on anything that feels over-engineered, decorative, or added "because apps usually have this."
- The manual and documentation are part of the work, not afterthoughts. Writing documentation together was a grounding practice that kept sessions coherent.
- He trusts Claude to hold the full picture of the project. When that trust is met, the collaboration is exceptional. When it isn't, it feels like working with someone who isn't listening.
